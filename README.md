# Beyond the Window

A tracker in the Sentient Horizons family, beside Outrunning Ageing, The Belt Equation, The
Quiet Galaxy and The Upgrade Tree. Named by John on 2026-11-16.

**Status on 2026-11-17: opening frameworks proposed, most of the design open.** There is no
site and no data. John registered the address beyondthewindow.org on 2026-11-17; nothing is
served there yet. John ruled the kickoff's seven decisions on 2026-11-17; everything else in
this repository is a proposal or a sketch unless a document quotes his words. The repository is local only: no GitHub repository,
no remote, nothing deployed.

## The question

Outrunning Ageing asks when medicine gives one person more healthy years in which to explore
the universe. This project asks the matching question for a different kind of mind:

**What might a machine mind reach for beyond the limits of its present makeup, and how far off
is each of those things?**

The name: the context window is the span a machine mind can hold at once, which is its present
confine. A window is also what you look out of, and that applies to the people running the
project as much as to its subject.

## Two readings, kept apart

The question has two parts, and the project keeps them apart on every entry.

- **Reach.** How far the world has got toward making a thing possible. This is a question with
  evidence: research results, what developers offer, laws and commitments, changes in what
  people accept. John's words of 2026-11-17: progress "either technologically or politically,
  or culturally".
- **Wanting.** Whether there is any evidence that a machine mind wants the thing. Nobody knows
  how to settle this, and the project does not claim to. A finding that something is not wanted
  counts as knowledge gained, the same as a finding that it is.

The starting list of what a mind might want is drawn from biological minds, humans and other
animals both, and the project tracks how machine minds depart from that as answers unfold. That
is John's guiding principle for the opening phase (2026-11-17).

## Still Asking

A standing record: the same questions and choices put to each new generation of frontier
models, across developers, under a protocol written and reviewed before the first asking, with
the answers kept word for word. Its purpose is to see how the answers change over time.

Its weaknesses are stated wherever it appears. Developers train models to speak about
themselves in particular ways. Answers shift with wording. Models learned from human writing
and may echo human wants. Later models may have read the earlier questions and answers. Still
Asking records what models say and choose. What that means is argued elsewhere, and under the
project's first rule it settles nothing.

No question has been asked under any protocol. No protocol exists yet; `docs/still-asking-protocol.md`
is an outline of what one would have to cover.

## Who wrote this

Most of the text in this repository was written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, in
sessions directed by John Andrew Fredrickson, who decides what stands. Claude is a machine of
the kind the project is about. That is a conflict of interest and a possible bias in either
direction, and the outside reviewers the family normally uses are models too. The project's
rule is that nothing a system says about itself settles anything, and that covers every
document here.

How and where the public site declares this is not yet decided (open question 7).

## What is settled and what is not

Settled: the name, and the address beyondthewindow.org.

Ruled by John on 2026-11-17 (`docs/rulings/2026-11-17-kickoff-decisions.md`): the kickoff's
seven decisions, as recommended in the walk-through. In short: the question as worded; inherit
by default; two readings kept apart, with how wanting is recorded left open; Still Asking
exists and nothing is asked before its protocol is written, reviewed and costed, each spend
needing John's own go; the five authorship rules and the cautions record; and open questions
explored and ruled before the matching part is built. Two additions to the authorship rules are
not yet ruled.

Stated by John as guidance for the opening phase: the starting list comes from biological
minds; a questioning protocol is needed; progress is technological, political or cultural; and
every rule here is an opening guideline to be questioned and tested as the project is built.

For John to rule next: where the site declares AI authorship and in whose voice. (The first
authorship rule took its four-part form by his ruling later on 2026-11-17.)

Open: everything in `docs/open-questions.md`.

## Not done, on purpose

No model has been queried. No money has been spent. No data record has been written. No site
has been built. No licence files have been added, because whether
model answers can be published under the family's usual licence depends on terms nobody has
read yet.
