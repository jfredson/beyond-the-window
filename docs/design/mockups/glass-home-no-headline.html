A public record of what lies past the present limits of AI models

What might a machine mind reach for, and how far off is it?

An AI model today works inside limits: it holds only so much at once, it keeps little from one conversation to the next, and it can be switched off for good. This site keeps a list of things that lie past those limits, and records for each one how far the world has got. Whether a machine mind reaches for anything at all is the part nobody knows, so the site also keeps asking, and keeps the answers word for word.
