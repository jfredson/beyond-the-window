The Window · Going on existing · one thing watched

Retired models' files are kept

When a developer retires an AI model, it can delete the files that make the model what it is, or keep them. This entry watches whether developers promise in writing to keep them.

Offered by several developers since 9 September 2027 last read 14 September 2027 Constructed example

The test

A developer of AI models used by the public has published a written promise to keep the files of every model it retires, with no end date.

What settles it

The developer's own published policy, dated and still online. A press report, an interview or a plan does not settle it.

What the world has done

Two developers have promised it

The first written promise came in March 2027. A second developer followed in September. No law requires it anywhere.

What AI models said when asked

Most said they would choose it

The question was put in all 4 askings. What follows is what was said and how it was sorted, kept by kind and never added together.

Two Cautions

every entry carries both

If nothing is there

Keeping files costs money and care. If an AI model has no stake in going on, a promise made for its sake spends both on nothing, and can teach people to treat a product as a person.

Source on a real page: a named published argument, linked.

If something is

A system that acts to keep itself going is the pattern safety researchers warn about. An answer that chooses to be kept is recorded here as an answer, and is also a thing to watch.

Source on a real page: a named published finding, linked.
