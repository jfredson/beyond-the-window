## Summary

The three document edits are careful. Every one is an added, dated note naming its ruling, the
quoted ruling words match their sources, and the correction of fact about the sealed capture is
right (ten questions to one model on 2026-11-19 and to a second on 2026-11-20, as the Still
Asking page says). Three notes write a step that no ruling took as if it were ruled.

The proposal is honest about its conflict of interest, keeps every mark with John, and quotes
its sources accurately with one exception. Its weak points are in the choices: three lack a
reason for their confidence, several would settle open questions without naming them, one rests
on a fact from memory that the project's own records contradict, and the second track leaves
out how the asking would actually be done.

## Must fix

**M1. Three choices are missing a reason for their confidence.** The family's rule is that
every choice put to John carries the choice, the confidence with a reason, standard practice or
judgment call, and the strongest alternative with its cost. Choice 6 (removal studies and a
related model) says only "*Confidence:* moderate." Choice 7 (condition 4's rates) says only
"*Confidence:* high." Choice 9 (a departure and the line on training) says only
"*Confidence:* moderate." Choice 12 gives "low on the three months" with no reason, though the
reflection section at the end supplies one ("nothing behind them but a guess"), which should be
moved into the choice. The other nine choices carry all four things.

**M2. Several choices would settle open questions without saying so.** Guideline 6 lets a
session recommend an answer to an open question, but John must be told which question his yes
would close. These choices do not name them:

- *Choice 1* (what a card describes: the exact model version in the first track, the named
  individual in the second) answers, for Still Asking, open question 1 ("Whose aspirations?":
  a set of trained weights, a character across versions, or a single conversation). The
  protocol edit itself says "The unit question above also decides what the card describes",
  but the choice does not name the question.
- *Choice 11* (how the first track runs) decides when an asking happens (on each new release,
  and again before a retirement), which is open question 12 ("Pace"); asks only through the
  programming interface, which is part of open question 16 ("Which models are asked, how, and
  on what terms"; apps only, direct only, or both); and tells the model the answers may be
  published, which is part of open question 10 ("The ethics of asking") and bears on open
  question 15 (publishing without spoiling the questions). Part 5 says open questions 3 and 10
  are "not settled here", but putting choice 11 to John as written would settle parts of them.
- *Choice 12* ("offered for real and honoured") takes one of open question 10's listed options
  ("No offers that will not be honoured").
- *Choice 13* (what a met mark does: shown, and nothing more) is the core of open question 19
  ("When does asking start to count?"), one of whose options is "the five as the rule for how
  much weight a Still Asking answer is given".

Each of these choices should name the open question, say which part it would close, and say
what stays open.

**M3. Choice 1 rests on a fact from memory that the project's records contradict.** Part 4
says of the first track: "That version does not change after release, so evidence about it
found later still applies". No source is given and it is not on the verify list. The project's
own list of family practice records "A provider changed under a run", where a preview model
"may drift under the provider" (`docs/inheritance.md`, part 2, item 47), and open question 16
repeats that a provider can change a model under a run. The sentence should be sourced from
each covered developer's own page on version pinning, or entered on the verify list and
softened ("a pinned version is meant not to change"), and choice 1's confidence should say the
premise is unchecked.

## Should fix

**S1. The four marks for conditions 1 to 3 come from an unruled description and are not put to
John.** The proposal says "Met", "not met" and "not measured" come from the third-reading
ruling's "What was put to him". That section describes the proposal's original five, and the
ruling's "What is ruled" section names no marks. The card ruling names only "partly met" (for
condition 1) and "not measured". Choices 4 and 6 extend "partly met", but no choice asks John
whether conditions 1 to 3 take a four-mark scale at all, and condition 3's marks ("met or not
met only from such a study") are not in any choice. Either add this as a short choice before
choice 4, or fold it into choice 4 so it covers all three conditions.

**S2. Choice 11 bundles five decisions, and its strongest alternative answers only one.** It
decides which models (only the one each developer names as its most capable generally
available model, which leaves out smaller models a developer ships at the same time), when, by
what route, with what instructions, and what the model is told. The alternative given (a fixed
calendar, twice a year) answers only "when". Either split it, or give the strongest
alternative for "which models" (every model a developer offers through its programming
interface) and for "what route" (the public app as well, which is open question 3's third
option) beside it.

**S3. Choice 12 leaves out how the second track would be asked, and what it commits John to.**

- The site's own entry on memory across conversations records memory as a feature of the
  developers' chat products ("in its chat product", in each announcement it cites). So a
  continuing individual would most likely be asked through a chat app, by hand, not by the
  pinning script Part 5 describes for the first track. The proposal does not say how, and does
  not raise whether each developer's terms allow that use, which the sealed capture ruling
  treated as a separate question needing an answer before any asking.
- "A separate account opened for the record" is an account only John can create, probably with
  a paid plan. The choice should say so, with the spending rule.
- "Offered for real and honoured" commits John, or the project, to act on a model's choice.
  The choice should say that plainly and give one constructed example of an option that could
  be offered and honoured, so John can judge whether any such option exists.

**S4. Part 5 says the first track feeds condition 4 through repeat askings, which Part 2 rules
out.** "What it feeds. The card's conditions 4 and 5 for that version, through repeat askings
and rewordings". Part 2 says condition 4 is fed only by published studies of a system's insides
and by the second track's checks against later behaviour. Repeat askings and rewordings feed
condition 5. The sentence should say condition 5 only, plus every answer's line on training.

**S5. A quote is credited to the ruling when it was the session's own words.** Part 5 says an
empty second track is "the ruling's own expectation ('a stated intention with nothing in it')".
Those words are in the two-tracks ruling, but in the session's confidence statement under "What
was put to him", not in what John ruled. Write "as the session said when the two tracks were put
to John".

**S6. Choice 2 does not say whether the card tells a reader that another developer's model read
the mark.** Step 3 sends any mark above the lowest to a model from another developer. John's
ruling of 2026-11-19 says disclosure of AI involvement belongs where a model is asked the
questions that make up the record's data, and the workspace rule keeps disclosure where a reader
needs it to judge a figure. A model reading a mark sits between the two. The choice should say
which way the proposal leans and leave it to John. It should also say that the reader would be
a Google or OpenAI model, since a Claude session writes the sheets, so the reader is always a
model from one of the three developers the record covers. The proposal says the reader "is
itself a subject" but not that this holds for every possible reader. And it should note that no
key exists for this use, and that the Google key was set up for the capture.

**S7. Choice 9 treats "most informative" as a label already on the page.** The choice says a
departure "keeps the label 'most informative'". The two-rewordings ruling says "No label or
sentence for a public page is written or ruled", and its own correction notes that "a departure
is the most informative" was the earlier proposal's step and not its source's. The choice
should say "is treated as the most informative result, in words not yet ruled".

**S8. The order of the choices.** The proposal says the later choices lean on the earlier, but:

- Choice 10 (where a statement checked against later behaviour goes in the order of evidence)

- Choice 13 (what a met mark does) frames how much care every other mark needs, and it is open

Two pairs could be merged, bringing the count to eleven: choices 3 and 5 (choice 5 follows from
choice 3's refusal to mislead a model about being watched), and choices 8 and 9 (both are the
