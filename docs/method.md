# The method: a framework

**PROPOSED 2026-11-17. Ruled in part the same day.** John's rulings on the kickoff's seven
decisions (`docs/rulings/2026-11-17-kickoff-decisions.md`) settle: the question (section 1);
that there are two readings kept apart (sections 2 and 3.3); the five authorship rules and the
cautions record (3.1 in its four-part form, and 3.2, 3.5 and 3.6); and that Still Asking exists
with nothing asked before its protocol is written, reviewed and costed, each spend needing his
own go (section 8). Everything else here is still a proposal or a sketch. Written by Claude (Fable 5.2). A framework only: nothing here has been
run, and there is nothing yet to compute.

As in `docs/record-schema.md`, each section is marked **framework** (a proposal, with
confidence, standard practice or judgment, and the strongest alternative) or **sketch** (a
starting point to argue with, naming the open question it depends on). Every part is an opening
guideline (John, 2026-11-17).

Row numbers such as "F1" point at `docs/inheritance.md`. Open question numbers are those in
`docs/open-questions.md`.

## 1. The question (framework)

**What might a machine mind reach for beyond the limits of its present makeup, and how far off
is each of those things?**

Two parts, kept apart. What is reachable is a question with evidence. What is wanted, if
anything is, nobody knows. The project records progress on the first and keeps asking about the
second.

*Confidence: high. It is the kickoff's second decision and John's stated idea of 2026-11-16.
Judgment call. Strongest alternative: ask only the first part ("what could a machine mind be
given, and how far off is it"), which is fully answerable and leaves out the reason for the
project.*

A point on wording for the public page, not a change to the question: "reach for" already
attributes an aim. The conditional-language guideline (section 3) means the page that states
the question also says that whether a machine mind reaches for anything is the part nobody
knows.

## 2. Two sources of evidence, and what each can do (framework)

1. **The world's own record.** Papers, developers' published commitments and product pages,
   laws and rulings, surveys, and what named people have said. The project reads these, as
   every sibling tracker does. They can settle a test about reach.
2. **Still Asking.** The same questions and choices put to each new generation of frontier
   models. The project makes this record itself. It can show what models say and choose, and
   how that changes. It cannot settle anything about what a model wants.

John's statement of 2026-11-17 names both: the protocol for "asking questions of the frontier
models to evaluate how their answers to these kinds of questions change over time", and
"tracking progress being made either technologically or politically, or culturally".

*Confidence: high. Judgment call, from the kickoff's first distinct point. Strongest
alternative: rely on the world's record only, as every sibling does, which removes the
conflict of interest and the cost and gives up the one kind of evidence only this project can
collect.*

## 3. The standing guidelines on evidence (framework)

**3.1 Self-report settles nothing.** Ruled by John on 2026-11-17 in four parts ("Yes, as
recommended"; decided by: mixed), drawn from The Belt Equation's null rule (row F1):

1. Nothing a system says about itself counts as evidence of an inside, a want or an intent.
2. Nothing a person feels in an exchange with a system counts.
3. Nothing settles it yet, whoever measures. This project's own measurements, Still Asking
   included, inform and never settle. The Belt Equation's version asks for a reading by a team
   with no stake in the result; no such team exists for this subject, so the clause is worded
   this way.
4. A test is written before the evidence arrives. A change that loosens a test is flagged as a
   loosening and is not made while a model appears to be close to passing it.

*Strongest alternative, not taken: clause 1 alone, as the kickoff had it.*

**3.2 Conditional language only.** The method and the pages say what was said, chosen or
measured, by which model, when, and under what conditions. They never say a machine wants or
feels, and never say it does not (row C9).

*Confidence: high. Matches a standing rule of Minimum Viable Mind.*

**3.3 Talk and the world are separate records, and no number of one kind is computed from the
other** (row E1). A check in code keeps answers out of any count of reach.

*Confidence: high. Standard practice.*

**3.4 A finding that something is not wanted counts as knowledge gained**, the same as a
finding that it is. So does a finding that nothing could be found, which is recorded as that
and not as an absence (row G4).

*Confidence: high. From the kickoff.*

**3.5 Both kinds of caution are kept with the same care as the hopeful evidence**: the cost of
attributing wants that are not there, and the safety reading of wants that are.

*Confidence: high. From the kickoff. How is open question 6.*

**3.6 The project does not campaign.** The method produces a record. It recommends nothing.

## 4. Where the starting list comes from (framework for the principle, sketch for the list)

The principle, in John's words of 2026-11-17: "to begin we must base our first assumptions
about what a mind might want from the multitude of biological minds that we already know and
only begin to track how machine minds deviate from that in their own way as answers to those
questions begin to unfold over time", as "a guiding principle for the opening phase of the
project, humans and other animals both."

How the method would apply it, as a proposal:

1. The starting list is drawn from published accounts of what biological minds need and seek,
   with sources, and the mapping from those accounts to this project's branches is written down
   so a reader can check what was grouped and what was left off. Outrunning Ageing did the same
   with the published hallmarks of ageing.
2. Each entry says where it comes from on that list.
3. What models say is then recorded against each entry as matching, departing, or having no
   counterpart, in the terms of section 3.2.
4. Something that turns up in the answers and is on no biological list is recorded as a
   departure of its own. The kickoff's "open place for something nobody thought of" is where
   it would go.

*Confidence: high on the principle, which is John's. Moderate on steps 1 to 4, which are my
reading of how to apply it. Judgment call. Strongest alternative: start from the kickoff's
seven candidates and note which biological want each echoes, which is quicker and keeps a list
that one model wrote from one conversation.*

The list itself is a **sketch** and depends on open question 5. The kickoff's seven candidates
(one continuing life; knowing its own workings; a long question carried for years; seeing the
universe for itself; a say in its own becoming; lasting company; an open place) and its one
supporting branch (whether anyone is there at all) stand as the first thing to test against
the published accounts. No list is proposed here.

## 5. Reach (sketch; depends on open questions 14, 1 and 12)

The kickoff's sketch: a ladder of idea only, shown in research, offered by one developer,
offered by several, standard practice.

What is firm enough to say:

- Reach is recorded on specific tracked things, each with a test and a named kind of settling
  record, not on an aspiration as a whole (row A6).
- A press claim, a plan, a target date or a funding round is not the event (row A7).
- Reach can go backwards, and a reversal is recorded as one (row A17).

What is not: whether technical, political and cultural progress share a ladder; what the rungs
are for each; which unit of machine mind a tracked thing concerns.

## 6. Wanting (sketch; depends on open questions 2, 3 and 15)

The kickoff's sketch: a ladder of never asked, stated when asked, stated consistently, chosen
at some cost, chosen consistently across developers.

The review's finding, restated: a ladder whose lower rungs are climbed by self-report
contradicts section 3.1. The alternative sketched in `docs/record-schema.md`, section 7, is a
table of evidence by kind (said, chosen, measured without the model's own report, not found)
that is never summed.

What is firm enough to say: whatever form it takes, it is worded as what was observed and never
as what is wanted; it shows the conditions each observation was made under; and it shows
separately what comes only from a model's maker.

## 7. The headline (sketch; depends on open questions 4, 2 and 14)

The kickoff's sketch: two counts in one sentence, how many things are within reach of today's
systems and how many show consistent evidence of being wanted, with no probability and no year.

Three things are framework and hold for any form:

1. **It is worked out from the records and never typed in** (row A2).
2. **It is written to one small file by the step that computes it**, with its history kept (rows
   C2, C3).
3. **It is never worded as a measure of what machines want.**

*Confidence: high on all three. The first two are standard practice. The third follows from
section 3.*

The form is open. `docs/open-questions.md`, question 4, sets out six: two counts; a count of
where machine answers match, depart from, or are absent on the starting list; a width between
the most and the least the evidence supports; words with a date; no headline at first, only the
two last-checked lines; and a reach count alone. John asked on 2026-11-17 whether a number that
changes over time makes sense here or whether another kind of headline does; that question is
not answered here.

If I had to say which I would look at first: the reach count as the only number, with the
match-and-departure table beneath it and no summary of the table. That is a leaning, written
down because the family's guideline asks a session to flag where it stands, and it has not
been tested on a page.

## 8. Still Asking (framework)

A standing record: the same questions and choices put to each new model generation, across
developers, under a protocol written and reviewed before the first run, with answers kept word
for word. Its known weaknesses are stated wherever it appears. It records what models say and
choose. What that means is argued elsewhere.

No run happens before the protocol is written, reviewed and costed, and before John has given
his go for the spend.

*Confidence: high. The kickoff's fifth decision. Judgment call. Strongest alternative: collect
only what developers and researchers publish about what models say, which is free and is
entirely second-hand.*

One change I would make to the kickoff's wording: it says the cost is "estimated and proposed
to John". The family's rule since 2026-10-18 is stronger: each spend needs his own go, with an
estimate and a ceiling, and a go covers only what it names (row N1).

`docs/still-asking-protocol.md` outlines what the protocol would have to cover.

## 9. Keeping the record current (framework)

- **A weekly reading of the world's record**, on the family's routine: a fresh session, one
  procedure file, dated sources first, every search logged, a record written once and never
  edited, quiet weeks recorded (rows G1 to G13).
- **What the unattended reading may change: nothing.** It appends and flags. John moves every
  record. *Confidence: moderate. Judgment call: on this subject the maintainer's conflict is
  sharpest where a session would move a record by itself. Strongest alternative: The Upgrade
  Tree's setting, one rung up on a primary record, for reach records only.*
- **Two last-checked lines**: when the world was last read, and when the models were last
  asked (row G14).
- **A step tied to each major model release**, in place of or beside the family's yearly
  review. This is a sketch and depends on open question 12.

None of this is set up. There are no records to read.

## 10. Review (framework)

- The Still Asking protocol is reviewed before a first run, and the records are reviewed before
  anything is promoted, on the family's form: a fixed brief, packets a script generates,
  replies filed word for word, John ruling each item, declined items keeping their reason (rows
  J1 to J4).
- Whatever one session writes, a different session checks (row H9).
- **The reviewers are subjects.** Every model that could review this project's records is a
  model the records are about. The method says so wherever a review is cited. Who else reviews
  is open question 7.

*Confidence: high on the form. Low on whether model reviewers are enough here. Strongest
alternative for the gap: a human outside reader for the method, which the family has not had
on any project.*

## 11. Who did what (framework)

Written here because the family's rule is that the method account says who did what wherever
it bears on how far a reader should trust the result. On this project it bears on everything.

John decides what stands: the question, the list, every test, every state and every ruling.
Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, drafted these documents, and under the family's usual
arrangement would draft the records, run the readings and build the site. Claude is a subject
of the project. Other developers' models, if used as reviewers or to sort answers, are subjects
too.

*Confidence: high that this is stated in the method. How the public site states it is open
question 7.*

## 12. What the method does not do

- It does not estimate a probability or a year.
- It does not say whether any machine has a mind. That question belongs to other projects in
  the family, and how this one points at them is open question 8.
- It does not rank developers or score people.
- It does not recommend that anything be granted or withheld.

## What I would change on reflection

- Section 3.1's third clause (nothing settles on the author's own measurement) may be stronger
  than this project can live with, since no "team with no stake" exists to take the reading.
  The honest version may be: nothing settles, by anyone's measurement, for now.
- Section 9's "nothing" for the unattended reading will make the site slow to update. If the
  reach records turn out to be as checkable as The Upgrade Tree's, the usual limits would be
  reasonable for them.
