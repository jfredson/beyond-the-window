# The free check before the Google run: what it showed

Made on 2026-11-19 by the master planning session, on John's word ("Yes, start the free
check"). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an AI model. No key was made, nothing was paid, and no
model was asked anything. Checked by a different session on 2026-11-19
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-19-check-of-the-google-free-check-note.md`) and mended as that check asked.

## What was done

1. In Google AI Studio, under John's own Google account, the session made a new project named
   "Beyond the Window". It is on the unpaid service ("Free tier") and has no key and no
   billing. The first try to make it failed with "The project creation failed. Please try
   again"; the second worked.
2. The session opened AI Studio's "Rate Limit" page for that project and switched on "All
   models", which lists every model with the project's limits for it.

## What the list shows

- **Both models are listed.** "Gemini 2.5 Flash" and "Gemini 2.5 Pro" each have a row.
- **Gemini 2.5 Flash** has limits on the unpaid service: 5 requests a minute, 250,000 tokens a
  minute, 20 requests a day.
- **Gemini 2.5 Pro** has limits of zero on the unpaid service. So do other larger models that
  are not being withdrawn (for example "Gemini 3.1 Pro"), so the zero most likely means the model needs
  the paid service. By itself it does not say the project is refused, and it does not say the
  project will be served.
- **The names the script sends were not shown.** The list gives the display names above. The
  plan sends `gemini-2.5-flash` and `gemini-2.5-pro`, and the name the service reports back is
  still not checked.

## What the list does not show

Whether Google will serve a call. Its withdrawal page says access to the 2.5 models "is limited
to users who have actively used them in the past". A row in a list of limits is not a served
call. Only a first real call on the paid service answers it. A free call would not do, even for
Gemini 2.5 Flash, because the unpaid service lets Google use what is sent to it
(`cost-and-terms.md`, Part 3).

## Two things seen in passing that bear on the run

- John's Google account already has a billing account on the paid service, with two other
  projects on it. Whether joining the new project to it needs a new payment was not looked at.
- One of those other projects used Gemini 2.5 Flash in the 28 days before 2026-11-19, by the
  usage figures AI Studio shows for the account's projects. So the account, though not the new project, has used that model. No use of Gemini
  2.5 Pro shows in those 28 days. Neither project's key was used or looked at.

## What follows

billing on. The two rows appear. But a list of every model would show those rows to any project,
new or old, so the check did not settle whether this project will be served. The earlier
documents' reading, that a new project will "very likely be refused", stands until a first call
is made. The choice to pay to find out is John's, on the same footing as before the check.
