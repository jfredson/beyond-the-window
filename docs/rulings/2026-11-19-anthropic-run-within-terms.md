# Ruling: the Anthropic run of the sealed capture is within Anthropic's terms, as far as John is concerned

Ruled by John on 2026-11-19, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session read the terms and recommended, John decided as the customer). Written by
Claude (Fable 5.2), an AI model.

## How it came up

The step list (`docs/capture/what-john-needs-to-do.md`) leaves to John, as the customer who
agrees to a developer's terms, whether each run is within them. This was the second of six
decisions put to him before the run that asks Claude Sonnet 4.5, which Anthropic withdraws on
2026-11-30.

## What the session told him

From `docs/capture/cost-and-terms.md`, Part 3, written by a session that opened the pages:

- **Which contract applies.** Anthropic's Commercial Terms of Service
  (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms), which say they govern a customer's use of
  Anthropic keys. John's existing Console organization already works under them.
- **Keeping the answers.** The terms say the customer "owns its Outputs". Nothing the session
  read limits storing them.
- **Asking these questions.** The terms point to the Usage Policy
  (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup). The session read its section on abuse of the platform
  in full and searched the rest, and found nothing that forbids asking a model open questions
  about itself. The nearest rule forbids getting round a model's safeguards, and the ten
  questions do not do that.

What was not settled, and was said to him:

- The terms say they are "not for consumer use". The session read this as no obstacle.
- The Service Specific Terms and the Data Processing Addendum, which the terms bring in by
  reference, have not been opened by anyone.
- Whether the terms allow publishing a model's answers word for word has not been looked for.
  The run only asks and keeps, so this does not bear on the run. It is checked before anything
  is published (`docs/verify-list.md`, entry 14).

The recommendation, with its four things. *The choice:* yes, within terms. *Confidence:* high;
this is ordinary paid use of the service. *Standard practice or judgment call:* standard
practice. *Strongest alternative:* have a session open the two unread documents first, which
costs about an hour and no money and guards against a small risk. The session offered to do
both.

The question put to him was "Is the Anthropic run within Anthropic's terms as far as you are
concerned?" John: "Yes, within terms". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The run that asks Claude Sonnet 4.5 the ten approved questions, five times each, and keeps the
replies is within Anthropic's terms as far as John is concerned.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **Publishing.** Nothing here says the terms allow publishing the answers.
- **Google's and OpenAI's terms**, which are separate decisions for their own runs.
- **The two unread documents.** John did not ask for them to be opened first. They stay listed
  as unopened in `docs/capture/cost-and-terms.md`.
- The key, the command that loads it, and the go to spend, which are all still John's.

No model has been asked anything, no key has been handled, and nothing has been spent.
