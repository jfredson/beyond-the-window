# Ruling: short passages from John's unpublished book and working files may be public in the reading report

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
John**, against the session's recommendation. Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an AI model.

## How it came up

The reading of the five conditions against their sources
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-20-the-five-conditions-read-against-their-sources.md`) quotes about
twenty-five short passages from chapters and appendices of The Calibration Problem, and from
the positions record and an editorial working page of Sentient Horizons. Those are kept in two
private repositories, and this repository is public. The report also gives the gist of two
positions that it says have no published source. A checking session raised it
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-20-check-of-the-rulings-on-a-machines-word.md`), and nothing was pushed
until John answered.

## What was put to him

The session recommended taking the passages out, with the full version kept in the folder that
stays out of the repository. *Confidence:* high, because the book is unpublished. *Strongest
alternative:* let them be public, since they are short, they are his own words, and an earlier
public proposal already quotes two phrases of the book, at the cost that passages of an
unpublished book would be on GitHub before the book is out.

The question put to him was "Keep the passages from your unpublished book out of the public
repository?" John: "They can be public".

## What is ruled

The reading report is pushed as written, with its quotations.

## Not covered by this ruling

Any other passage of the book or of the working files. A later document that would quote them
is put to John before it is committed, not after.
