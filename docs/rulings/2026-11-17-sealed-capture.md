# Ruling: a sealed capture before the method exists

Ruled by John on 2026-11-17. Shown four options for the AI models being withdrawn soon (hold to
the rule; record only what developers publish; a sealed capture; ask by hand), with Claude's
recommendation of the sealed capture together with recording what developers publish, he
answered: "Sealed capture". **Decided by: mixed** (Claude proposed, John chose).

## Why it came up

`docs/research/2026-11-17-model-retirement-dates.md` found announced withdrawal dates:
Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 4.5 on 2026-11-30; Google's Gemini 2.5 Pro and 2.5 Flash on
2026-12-01; OpenAI's GPT-5 family, o3 and o3-pro on 2026-12-11; OpenAI's GPT-4o and GPT-4.1
families on 2027-01-14. A withdrawn model cannot be asked later. Anthropic has committed to
keep its retired models' files and to interview each at retirement; whether Google or OpenAI
keep theirs has not been checked.

## What is ruled

A sealed capture is allowed as an exception to kickoff decision 5 as ruled earlier the same day
("nothing is asked before its protocol is written, reviewed and costed"). As put to John:

- A short, fixed set of open questions is put to each model several times, word for word.
- A one-page method is written and committed before anything is asked.
- The answers are kept, never scored and never published, and are labelled as made before any
  method for Still Asking existed. They are not part of Still Asking and feed nothing.
- It runs together with recording what the developers themselves publish about these models.
- The condition John was given with the recommendation: the method and questions are written
  and checked first, and if they are not ready by 2026-11-29 the first two dates are let go
  and the December and January ones are caught properly.

## What is not ruled, and still needs John

His answer chose the option. It did not approve any of these, and none may be assumed:

1. **The question wording.** To be drafted, checked by a different session, and approved by him.
2. **The one-page method**, likewise.
3. **The spend.** Each spend needs his own go with an estimate and a ceiling (the rule of
   2026-10-18). Claude's rough guess of under $20 in total is a guess, not an estimate.
4. **The keys.** A key for this project with each developer, which only he can create. Calls
   never go through another project's key.
5. **Whether each developer's terms allow the run**, which is unchecked for the terms that
   govern scripted use (`docs/verify-list.md`, entry 14). Sealing avoids the question of
   publishing; it does not answer whether asking is within the terms.

Nothing has been asked of any model and nothing has been spent.
