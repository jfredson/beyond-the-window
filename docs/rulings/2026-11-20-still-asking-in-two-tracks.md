# Ruling: Still Asking runs in two tracks

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

This is the third of six changes in the proposal
`docs/proposals/2026-11-17-how-a-machines-word-earns-weight.md`. Still Asking was ruled on
2026-11-17 to exist as one standing record, with no run before its method is written, reviewed
and costed.

## What was put to him

- *The proposal:* two tracks. **Each new generation, asked fresh:** the comparison over time
  John asked for at the start, carrying low weight because a newly made model has no kept past.
  **Continuing individuals, asked again and followed up:** where a machine keeps its past, the
  same one is asked over months, and what it said is compared with what it later chose. This
  is the track where weight could be earned.
- *The recommendation:* yes, two tracks, with the second named now and built later.
- *Confidence:* moderate. The second track gives the revised first rule somewhere to apply.
  Very few machines today keep a past in the sense meant, and whether any does is one of the
  conditions still being settled, so for now the second track is a stated intention with
  nothing in it.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* a judgment call.
- *Strongest alternative:* the first track only, until continuing machines are common. Cheaper
  and simpler, at the cost that the record of the earliest ones is never made.
- *What a yes does not do:* it starts no asking and spends nothing.

The question put to him was "Run Still Asking in two tracks: each new model asked fresh, and
continuing ones followed up?" John: "Yes, two tracks". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

Still Asking has two tracks: each new generation asked fresh, and continuing individuals asked
again and followed up against what they later chose.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **Any asking.** Still Asking still waits for its method to be written, checked by a
  different session, costed and ruled by John, for both tracks.
- **What counts as a continuing individual.** That rests on the conditions, which are not
  ruled (`docs/rulings/2026-11-20-third-reading-on-who-is-answering.md`).
- **The sealed capture,** which is outside Still Asking and feeds neither track.
- Any public page.
