# Ruling: the two figures under the window drawing are dropped

Ruled by John on 2026-11-19, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

The home page's drawing is a window. Filled squares inside the frame are the things offered
today, and empty squares to the right of the frame are the things on the list not offered yet.
Under the drawing are two small figures, "5 inside the frame" and "3 outside it". John ruled on
2026-11-18 that the count stands with no total beside it
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-18-home-page-count.md`). The check of the site's text noted that the two
figures let a reader add them into the total that ruling removed, and a session left the point
for John (`docs/first-version-choices.md`, choice 3). The master session opened the home page
in the browser pane so that he could see it.

## What the session told him

- *The choice:* drop both figures and keep the squares, or keep both figures.
- *The recommendation:* drop both.
- *Why:* the count is already the largest thing on the page, and "3 outside it" reads as "3 to
  go". The empty squares stay, with the sentence under the drawing saying they are only the
  things found so far.
- *Confidence:* moderate to high.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* judgment call.
- *Strongest alternative:* keep both, as plain facts a reader can count from the squares
  anyway. Its cost is a total one sum away from the count.
- *A middle course, not recommended:* keep the first figure and drop the second.

The question put to him was "What happens to the two figures under the window drawing?" John:
"Drop both figures". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The two figures under the window drawing, "N inside the frame" and "N outside it", come off the
page. The squares, the key and the sentence under the drawing stay.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **The drawing's spoken summary for screen readers,** which gives the same two numbers in
  words. The session's view (decided by: agent) is that it keeps them, because a reader who
  cannot see the squares has no other way to learn what the drawing shows. It is put to John
  if he wants it otherwise.
- **The rest of the design proposal's points:** the central picture as such, the default
  theme, the two meaning-colours, the top bar and the skin's name.

A session makes the change with the fixes that follow the check of the site, and a different
session checks it. Nothing is published.
