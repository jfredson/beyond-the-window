# Ruling: every page is read against John's published positions before it goes out

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

This is the sixth and last change in the proposal
`docs/proposals/2026-11-17-how-a-machines-word-earns-weight.md`. It had two halves.

**The author's line needed no ruling.** The proposal asked for one line saying the site's
author argues positions elsewhere, with a link. The About page John approved on 2026-11-19
already says: "I argue for my own views in essays at Sentient Horizons, and not here." The
session told him so and did not ask him to rule on it.

## What was put to him

- *The proposal:* before any page goes out, it is read against John's record of published
  positions (`sentient-horizons/editorial/corpus-positions-ledger.md`), by the routine the blog
  uses (`sentient-horizons/editorial/working-positions-coherence.md`). The aim is to catch a
  sentence that contradicts something he has argued in print, or that quietly takes a side the
  site says it does not take.
- *Why here more than on the sibling sites:* several of his positions are not neutral on this
  subject, and a site that claims to recommend nothing can lean without anyone noticing.
- *Confidence:* high. The same day, five conditions that looked sound against summaries of his
  positions each needed rewording against the full text.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* standard on the blog; new for a tracker site, so a
  small departure from the sibling sites, with the reason above.
- *Strongest alternative:* not needed, since every page is already checked against its
  sources by a different session. The cost is that those checks compare a page with the world
  and none compares it with what John has said.
- *What it costs:* one more read by a session before a page is published, and no money.

The question put to him was "Have every page read against your published positions before it
goes out?" John: "Yes, check each page". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

A new or changed page is read against the positions record before it is published. That the
reader is a session that did not write the page is the session's addition, following the
project's standing practice. What was put to John said "one more read by a session".

## Not covered by this ruling

- **The pages already live.** The session said it would have them read once against the
  positions unless John said otherwise. That is the session's undertaking, not his ruling.
- **What happens when a page and a position disagree.** The reader reports it. Whether the
  page or the position gives way is John's.

## An exception, ruled 2026-11-21

For a page publishing the sealed capture's answers, the reader reads the page's own text against
the positions record and leaves the answers themselves out, so that no model reads them before
they are public. John: "Yes, as proposed", to choice 11 of the comparison method (decided by:
mixed; `docs/rulings/2026-11-21-sealed-capture-comparison-choices.md`).
