# Ruling: the memory entry says nothing is recorded yet under the second caution

Ruled by John on 2026-11-19, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

This is the third of the seven points John decides before a session writes the third caution
into the records (`docs/research/2026-11-18-third-caution-sources.md`, section 6, with the
detail in its section 1.5). The entry is the one about what a model learned in one conversation
being carried into the next (`memory-across-conversations`). John ruled on 2026-11-18 that this
entry sits beside the list and is not counted, because the memory it tracks is kept for the
user.

The developer's published document on its models' character has a passage that treats the lack
of memory as something that could bear on the model. The question was whether that passage
goes under the second caution, the one headed "If an AI system has wants, and they go
unnoticed".

## What the session told him

- *The choice:* the second heading on this entry says nothing is recorded yet, or it carries
  the developer's passage with its limits stated.
- *The recommendation:* nothing recorded yet, with the passage kept in the research note as a
  lead for a possible later entry about memory kept for the model's own sake.
- *Why:* the passage is about something other than what the entry tracks. It is also a
  developer's own statement with no outside source beside it, so the ruling made just before
  this one (`docs/rulings/2026-11-19-developer-reason-not-alone.md`) would keep it out anyway.
- *Confidence:* high. The session that wrote the research note and the session that checked it
  reached the same answer.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* standard practice. It is the rule the ruling of
  2026-11-18 on the third caution gave for an entry with no source.
- *Strongest alternative:* carry the passage. Its cost is a caution about a different subject
  from the one the entry is about.

The question put to him was "What does the memory entry say under the second caution?" John:
"Nothing recorded yet". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

1. **On the memory entry, the second caution says that nothing is recorded yet.**
2. **The developer's passage and the other leads stay in the research note,** for a later
   entry about memory kept for the model, if there is ever one.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **The exact sentence under the heading.** A session drafts it and a different session checks
  it.
- **Whether an entry about memory kept for the model is ever made.** Nothing here starts one.
- **Points 4 to 7.**

Nothing on the site has been changed by this ruling. No model has been asked anything, no key
has been handled, and nothing has been spent or published.
