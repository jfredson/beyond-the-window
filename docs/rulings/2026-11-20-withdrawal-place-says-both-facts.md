## How it came up

In the table of developers, the place for OpenAI in the row "A retired model stays open to the
public after its retirement date" read "Withdrawal: January 29th, 2026". The same notice that
announces the withdrawal from the chat product says: "In the API, there are no changes at this
time." So the model was withdrawn from one product and stayed open in another, and the place
said only the first.

## What was put to him

What the place should say. His answer, as relayed: "Say both facts (Recommended)".

## What is ruled

The place says that the model was withdrawn from the chat product and is still open to
developers, both from the same notice.

## How it is applied

The place reads: "January 29th, 2026: its notice says it will withdraw an older model from its
chat product, and that nothing changes for now in its service for developers. Not counted."
The wording is a session's. The entry's state is not changed: it still names one developer.
The act stays in the dated list, marked as an act that takes something back.

Not settled by this ruling, and not put to him: whether a model that is still open to
developers after leaving the chat product meets the entry's test. The developer's page on
retirements lists that model's versions as due to shut down on 2027-01-14.
