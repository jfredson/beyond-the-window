# Ruling: a third caution, the cost of seeing less than is there

Ruled by John on 2026-11-18, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John chose). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an AI
model. This ruling revises guideline 5 of the repository's instruction file, which John ruled on
2026-11-17 as part of the kickoff's decisions.

## What was put to him

Every entry records two cautions beside the hopeful evidence: the cost of seeing more in an AI
model than is there, and the worry about a system that does have wants of its own. Guideline 5
names exactly those two. The check of the site's text
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-17-site-text-against-positions.md`, finding 4) called the gap its most
serious finding: there is no place for the opposite mistake, the cost of seeing less in a model
than is there. A reader is shown one direction of error on every page and never the other; 13
of the 27 cautions recorded sit under "seeing more than is there", most of that text from one
essay arguing against work on model welfare; and John's published positions hold both mistakes
to be real. Because it changes a ruled guideline, a session could not make the fix.

He was shown three choices: a third caution with its own heading; widening the second caution
to "the cost of getting it wrong, in either direction"; or one sentence on the About page. The
session recommended the third caution. *Confidence:* high that the missing cost should be
added, moderate on a third heading over widening the second. *Standard practice or judgment
call:* judgment call, and a change to a ruled guideline. *Strongest alternative:* widen the
second, a smaller change to the pages that hides the imbalance it is meant to fix.

John: "Yes, a third caution". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

Three cautions are recorded on every entry, with the same care as the hopeful evidence:

1. the cost of attributing wants that are not there;
2. the cost of missing wants that are there;
3. the risk in wants that are there.

Guideline 5 is revised to say so, as a dated change that keeps the wording of 2026-11-17.

## What is not ruled

- **The wording of the headings.** The present heading, "The cost of seeing more than is
  there", assumes nothing is there, and "seeing less than is there" would assume something is.
  A session proposes wording for both that assumes neither, and John sees it.
- **Which sources stand under the new heading on each entry.** A session looks for them and
  cites only pages it opened that say the thing. Where it finds none, the entry says nothing is
  recorded yet.
- **Whether the search is finished before the site is released.** The session's view, given to
  John and not ruled, is that it should be, so the new heading does not go live empty on every
  entry.

Nothing on the site is changed by this file, and nothing is published or deployed.
