# Ruling: the About page is rewritten in the shape of the family's About pages

Ruled by John on 2026-11-19, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (John raised the fault and asked for the comparison; a session proposed the shape; John
approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an AI model.

## How it came up

John asked to see the About page's section then headed "Who Decides, and What Has Been
Checked". Of its opening, "I am John Andrew Fredrickson, and I decide what stands", he said:

> "The introduction makes no sense to me. For a new reader to the site, why do we open with
> "and I decide what stands"? What is the purpose of that opening?"

The session answered that the sentence was one half of a contrast with the statement that an
AI model drafted the text, which had been removed that morning, and offered a shorter opening.
Of that draft John said:

> "None of this is important. It sounds like responses to made up questions that no new reader
> will have in their mind. Can we review the other tracker sites about pages and figure out a
> way to write this one more inline with those?"

and then: "Review Sentient Horizons too".

## What the session found and told him

It read the About pages of The Belt Equation, The Quiet Galaxy and Sentient Horizons, and the
method pages of The Upgrade Tree and Outrunning Ageing.

- The family's pages open with John and his reason ("I'm John, and I write about AI, sentience
  and the spread of conscious life beyond Earth at Sentient Horizons", then the question, then
  "I wanted a place where the argument is laid out piece by piece, every number carries its
  source, and a new discovery moves the answer in public"). Then a short "Why the name?". Then
  how it works. Then what has been read or what has moved. Sentient Horizons opens with the
  question it began with.
- This site's page opens with a line saying everything is expected to change, states the
  question with nobody asking it, has a section on who decides and what John has ruled, which
  no sibling has, and ends with nine things that are not known.

**The shape proposed:**

1. Who is asking: John, what he writes about, the family of sites, the question, and what he
   wanted a place for. The sentence that he argues his own views at Sentient Horizons and not
   here folds into this part.
2. Why the name?
3. How it works: the present "How It Is Made" points, kept.
4. Where the sources come from: the facts now in the paragraph about two small camps.
5. What has been checked: the dated checks, kept short.
6. What is not known yet: kept, and trimmed.

The paragraph listing what John has ruled goes.

The session gave a draft of parts 1 and 2 and two cautions: the reason for asking is John's to
give, and the draft puts no motive in his mouth; and the name paragraph paraphrases what he
said on 2026-11-17, that the project explores "what is beyond the confines of the current state
of digital minds", with the window as the image for it.

The question put to him was "Rewrite the About page in the family's shape?" John: "Yes, rewrite
it". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The About page is rewritten in the six-part shape above, in the voice of the family's About
pages.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **The sentences.** They are in John's first person. A session drafts them, a different
  session checks them, and John reads them before the go to publish. He did not approve the
  draft of parts 1 and 2 word for word.
- **His reason for asking.** No session writes one for him.
- **Nothing ruled earlier is undone:** the general statement of both mistakes, the ties between
  sources told on the About page, and no mention of AI help in building all still hold in the
  new shape.

Nothing is published.

## Added the same day: the opening gives no personal reason

The session asked whether John wanted to give a sentence or two, in his own words, on why the
question matters to him, as The Belt Equation's page does, and said that otherwise the opening
would follow The Quiet Galaxy's, which says what the question is and what he wanted a place
for. John: "Follow The Quiet Galaxy's opening, no personal reason". **Decided by: mixed.**

So part 1, "Who is asking", is modelled on The Quiet Galaxy's About page
(`~/Code/quiet-galaxy/site/src/pages/about.astro`): who John is and what he writes about, the
family of sites, the question, and what he wanted a place for. It states no personal motive.
