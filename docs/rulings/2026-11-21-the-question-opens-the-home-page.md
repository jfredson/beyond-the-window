# Ruling: the founding question opens the home page, above the headline number

Ruled by John on 2026-11-21, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (John asked for the question at the top; the session set out what it changes and
recommended the layout, which John approved). Written by Claude (Opus 5.5), an AI model.

## How it came up

John wrote: ""What might a machine mind reach for beyond the limits of its present makeup, and
how far off is each of those things?" I think this needs to be the headline question at the top
of the landing page".

The home page at the time opened, in this order, with the short line "A dated record of how the
world is treating AI models", the large count of 5, the sentence saying what is counted and the
breakdown by developer, and only then the question, as the page title
(`site/src/components/Rebuilt.astro`).

## What was put to him

The session told him that this changes his ruling of 2026-11-20 that the page "opens with a
headline number" (`docs/rulings/2026-11-20-home-page-opens-with-a-headline-again.md`), and that
the sibling sites it checked (The Upgrade Tree, Outrunning Ageing, The Quiet Galaxy) set the
number and the question side by side on a computer and show the number first on a phone, so
this site would be the only one in the family to open with the question on every screen.

*Recommendation:* the question at the very top on every screen, with the short line saying what
the site is just above it, and the count, its sentence and the breakdown directly beneath,
unchanged. *Confidence:* moderate to high, because the cold-reader test
(`docs/proposals/2026-11-20-what-a-stranger-makes-of-the-site.md`) found readers could not say
what the count counted when they met it first. *Judgment call.* *Strongest alternative:* the
sibling layout, question and number side by side, number first on a phone, which keeps the
family alike but still has a phone reader meet the number first. The question was "Should the
question open the page on every screen, with the 5 and its lines directly beneath?" John: "Yes,
question first". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

- The founding question opens the home page on every screen, as its headline, with the short
  line saying what the site is just above it.
- The count of 5, the sentence saying what is counted and the breakdown by developer follow
  directly beneath, unchanged in wording and still worked out from the records.
- The rest of the 2026-11-20 ruling stands: the count is still the first figure on the page, so
  the site check that refuses a home page whose first figure is not the count still applies.

## Before it goes out

A writing session makes the change; a different session checks it and reads the page against
John's published positions; John sees pictures of the page on a phone and a computer before it
is pushed.
