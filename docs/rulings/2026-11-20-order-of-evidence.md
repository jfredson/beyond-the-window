# Ruling: evidence on every entry is shown in a fixed order and never summed

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

This is the fourth of six changes in the proposal
`docs/proposals/2026-11-17-how-a-machines-word-earns-weight.md`.

## What was put to him

- *The proposal:* evidence is always shown in this order, and never added up into a score:
  measured without the machine's word; reports checked against an independent reading of the
  system's workings; choices made at a cost; bare statements.
- *Why:* John's published positions expect knowledge of these systems to come "by removal and
  not by how the system talks", and without an order the most quotable evidence, the bare
  statement, tends to stand first.
- *Confidence:* high on a fixed order with bare statements last, moderate on where "choices
  made at a cost" sits.
- *A caution he was given:* the reading of the five conditions against their sources
  (`docs/reviews/2026-11-20-the-five-conditions-read-against-their-sources.md`) found that one
  source records systems paying costs to avoid shutdown with nothing known to be at stake. So
  a costly choice may be weaker evidence than its place suggests. The session's view was that
  the order can stand, and that the label on the page should say what a costly choice does
  and does not show.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* it follows the positions.
- *Strongest alternative:* no fixed order, at the cost that bare statements lead by default.

The question put to him was "Show evidence on every entry in a fixed order, strongest kind
first, and never summed?" John: "Yes, that order". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

On every entry, evidence is shown in this order and is never summed: (1) measured without the
machine's word; (2) reports checked against an independent reading of the system's workings;
(3) choices made at a cost; (4) bare statements.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **The label for a costly choice.** The caution above is the session's, and the wording that
  answers it is not written or ruled.
- **Any public page.** The method and the record format are brought into line by a writing
  session once the rest of the proposal is ruled, and any change to a public page goes to John
  as wording first.
