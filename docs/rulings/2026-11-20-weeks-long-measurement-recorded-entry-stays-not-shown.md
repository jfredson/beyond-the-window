public measure stops at hours", and its three places in the table said nothing was found.

## What was put to him

shown" (Recommended)", "Change it to shown", "Rewrite the test first".

## What is ruled

entry's state stays "not shown" and it is still not counted. The wording that said the best
public measure stops at hours is corrected, because it was no longer true.
