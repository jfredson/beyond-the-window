# Ruling: the Still Asking page goes out in the first version

Ruled by John on 2026-11-19, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

Still Asking is the name for a record that does not exist yet: the same questions put to each
new set of AI models, with every answer kept. The page says today that no model has been asked
anything, why not, what an answer will and will not count for, and the known weaknesses of any
record of this kind. Whether it goes out in the first version was one of the site decisions
left, and the ruling on the short path
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-19-first-version-short-path.md`) noted it had not been put to him.

## What the session told him

- *The choice:* the page goes out in the first version, or it is held back until there is a
  ruled method.
- *The recommendation:* it goes out.
- *Why:* by John's ruling the same day
  (`docs/rulings/2026-11-19-no-public-mention-of-ai-help-in-building.md`), the one disclosure
  that matters is when an AI model is asked the project's questions, and this page is where
  that disclosure lives. Once the sealed capture runs, the site needs a place to say so.
- *Confidence:* moderately high.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* judgment call.
- *Strongest alternative:* hold it back, so that the first version promises nothing it cannot
  yet deliver. Its cost is that a reader would see no account of the second half of the site's
  own question.
- *What follows:* the page's headline, "No AI model has been asked anything yet", changes on
  the day the capture runs. The session said it would have that sentence drafted and checked
  ahead of time.

The question put to him was "Does the Still Asking page go out in the first version?" John:
"Yes, it goes out". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The Still Asking page is part of the first version.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **The page's sentences,** which John sees before the go to publish.
- **What the page says once the sealed capture has run.** A session drafts it, a different
  session checks it, and John sees it. It must say plainly that ten questions were put to
  models about to be withdrawn, under no ruled method, and that the answers feed nothing.
- **Whether any answer is ever shown.** Nothing here decides it.

Nothing is published.
