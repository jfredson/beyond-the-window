# Ruling: the sentence beside the count on the home page

Ruled by John on 2026-11-19, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

John's ruling of 2026-11-18 (`docs/rulings/2026-11-18-home-page-count.md`) set the form of what
opens the home page, a count with no total beside it, and left the exact sentence unruled. The
page has shown a session's draft since, with the line "A first count. The wording of the
sentence above is not settled yet." under it.

## What the session told him

- *As drafted:* "5 things that lie past the limits an AI model ordinarily works within are
  offered today by at least one developer of AI models."
- *Shorter:* "5 things past an AI model's ordinary limits are offered today by at least one AI
  developer."
- *The recommendation:* the shorter one. It says the same thing in fewer words, and a reader
  can repeat it.
- *Confidence:* moderate. The session had not seen it on the page, and said a different session
  would need to check it.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* judgment call.
- *Strongest alternative:* as drafted, which had been checked twice and needed no further work,
  at the cost of a sentence few readers will repeat.

John: "Shorter version, and take the short path". **Decided by: mixed.** The second half of his
answer is recorded in `docs/rulings/2026-11-19-first-version-short-path.md`.

## What is ruled

The sentence beside the count reads: **"[count] things past an AI model's ordinary limits are
offered today by at least one AI developer."** The count is worked out from the records and
never typed in. Today it is 5.

## What follows

- The line under it saying the wording is not settled goes, and so does the matching line in
  the About page's list of what is not known yet.
- A session makes the change and a different session checks it on the built page.

## Not covered by this ruling

- The paragraph of limits under the sentence, and the rest of the home page's words.
- Whether what an AI model says or chooses is ever summarised at the top, which stays open
  under question 4 in `docs/open-questions.md`.

Nothing is published.
