# Ruling: continuity is recorded as what is kept, and a match with human wants is the least informative result

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

This is the fifth of six changes in the proposal
`docs/proposals/2026-11-17-how-a-machines-word-earns-weight.md`. The session told John it had
checked the thirteen records on the live site and that neither rewording changes a sentence
that is public on 2026-11-20. Both bind what is written next.

## What was put to him

1. **Continuity.** The founding list of things a machine might want includes "one continuing
   life". Proposed: it becomes a record of what is kept and what is built from what, making no
   claim that retiring a model is a harm to it, and no claim that it is not. *Why:* John's
   published positions hold that question open, and the heading quietly answers it.
2. **Matches and departures.** Proposed: where what a model says matches the starting list
   drawn from biological minds, the page labels the match as the least informative result,
   because models were trained on human writing. A departure from that list, or evidence from
   the system's structure, is labelled the most informative.

- *Confidence:* high on the first, moderate to high on the second, which the reading of the
  five conditions against their sources supports
  (`docs/reviews/2026-11-20-the-five-conditions-read-against-their-sources.md`).
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* both remove a conflict with published positions.
- *Strongest alternative:* the first alone, at the cost that when Still Asking begins the
  answers most likely to be quoted are the ones that sound most human, with nothing on the
  page saying why that is weak evidence.
- *A caution he was given:* "least informative" and "most informative" are strong labels on a
  public page, and the exact words a reader sees come to him before they go anywhere.

The question put to him was "Adopt the two rewordings: continuity as a record of what is kept,
and matches as the weakest result?" John: "Yes, both". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

1. Continuity is recorded as what is kept and what is built from what. No document or page
   says that a model's retirement is a harm to it, or that it is not.
2. A match between what a model says and the starting list drawn from biological minds is
   treated as the least informative result. A departure, or evidence from the system's
   structure, is treated as the most informative.

John's guiding principle of starting from biological minds is unchanged.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **The words a reader sees.** No label or sentence for a public page is written or ruled.
- **Any record on the live site,** none of which changes by this ruling.
