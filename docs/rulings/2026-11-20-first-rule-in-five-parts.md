# Ruling: the first rule takes five parts, so that a machine's word can earn weight

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model. A model wrote the proposal for when a model's word should count, and a model
recommended it. John was told so before he ruled.

## How it came up

The proposal `docs/proposals/2026-11-17-how-a-machines-word-earns-weight.md`, written at John's
asking, holds six changes. This is the first. The rule it changes is guideline 2 in
`CLAUDE.md`, "Self-report settles nothing", ruled by him in four parts on 2026-11-17.

## What was put to him

- *Part one gains four words:* nothing a system says about itself counts "on its own word". A
  report checked against an independent reading of the system's workings would be recorded as
  its own kind of evidence, showing that the system can read and report an internal state, and
  not that the state is felt.
- *A new fifth part:* the weight given to a machine's word rises as the machine meets
  conditions fixed in advance, each tested by something other than the machine's word, and
  none loosened while a machine looks close to meeting it.
- *Why:* the rule as written is right today and would be wrong forever, and it conflicts with a
  position already published on Sentient Horizons about checked self-reports.
- *Confidence:* high on the four words, moderate on the fifth part, which only makes sense with
  the five conditions that are the next decision, so a yes commits him to settling those.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* a judgment call, and a change to a rule he ruled.
- *Strongest alternative:* the four words alone, leaving the rule flat until a machine plainly
  outgrows it. Simpler, at the cost that the rule would then be rewritten under exactly the
  pressure the rule warns against.

The question put to him was "Change the first rule so a machine's word counts for nothing 'on
its own word', with a fifth part on weight?" John: "Yes, both changes". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The rule's wording of 2026-11-17 is kept on record. As revised:

- **(a)** What a model says about itself is logged, labelled as what it is, and never counted
  on its own word as evidence of an inside, a want or an intent. A report checked against an
  independent reading of the system's workings is recorded as its own kind of evidence: that
  the system can read and report an internal state, and not yet that the state is felt.
- **(b), (c), (d)** Unchanged.
- **(e)** The weight given to a machine's word rises as it meets conditions fixed in advance.
  Each condition is tested by something other than the machine's word, and under part (d) none
  is loosened while a machine appears close to meeting it.

- **The conditions.** The proposal's five (a kept past, mattering, a point of view, checked
  self-reports, wants that hold) are its second change and are not ruled. Until they are, part
  (e) raises no machine's weight and no answer is treated differently from before.
