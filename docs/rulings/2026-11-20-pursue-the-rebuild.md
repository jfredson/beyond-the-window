# Ruling: pursue the rebuild

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with a working session (not the master planning
session). **Decided by: mixed** (John asked for the premise to be re-examined; a session
proposed the design and two courses; John chose). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an AI model.

## How it came up

A cold-reader audit of the family's eight public sites on 2026-11-20 found that neither of two
readers could say what this site is about, or what it is telling them, from its first screen
(`docs/proposals/2026-11-20-what-a-stranger-makes-of-the-site.md`). Two mock-ups that kept the
present design were put to John. He answered:

> "I think perhaps we need to step back and re-evaluate the entire premise of what we are
> measuring and how we are presenting it. If we could start from scratch, how would we redesign
> this to meet the criteria we are measuring in this audit?"

The session proposed counting dated acts by named actors in place of things on a list, and
opening the home page with several readings that the world moves in place of one count. Two
mock-ups of that design (Options C and C2 in the proposal) were built and read by fresh readers.

## What was put to him

Two courses:

- **Pursue the rebuild.** It changes what the project counts and sets aside the rulings of
  2026-11-18 (the home page opens with a count and no fraction) and 2026-11-19 (the sentence
  beside the count).
- **Option A for now.** Keep both rulings and fix the wording around the count.

*Confidence, as given to him:* moderate. The rebuilt first screen was the only version where
readers could say what the site is telling them today, on two readers and one screen. No
version held an impatient reader, and that question was said to need real people.
*Standard practice or judgment call:* judgment call. *Strongest alternative:* Option A, which
changes little and keeps both rulings.

John: "Pursue the rebuild". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The project works toward the rebuilt design: a dated act by a named actor as the unit, and a
home page that opens with several readings in place of the single count. Design and building
go ahead on that footing.

## What is not ruled

- **The published site does not change yet.** Publishing waits for John's go, as always. Until
  he approves a rebuilt page, the live site keeps the count and the sentence as ruled on
  2026-11-18 and 2026-11-19, and the check of the built site keeps enforcing them.
- **The design itself.** The record layout, which readings stand at the top, how each is worked
  out, and every sentence are proposed in
  `docs/proposals/2026-11-20-rebuild-design.md` and none is ruled.
- **Whether the home page names a developer.** The session that did this work declared a
  conflict on it: every thing now counted is offered by Anthropic, which made the model.
- **Which developers the record covers.**
- **The founding question.** Nothing here changes it or what the project is for. Where it
  appears on the site is part of the design, not ruled.
- **Anything about Still Asking.**
