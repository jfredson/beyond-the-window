# Ruling: the choices in the comparison method for the sealed capture, one at a time

Ruled by John from 2026-11-21, in conversation with the master planning session. The choices are
those in `docs/proposals/2026-11-21-sealed-capture-comparison-method.md`, Part 6, drafted by a
writing session and found fit to put to John by a different session
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-21-check-of-the-sealed-capture-comparison-method.md`). Each is recorded
here as it is answered. Written by Claude (Opus 5.5), an AI model.

## How it came up

John asked why the sealed capture's answers are not read, scored or used, and then ruled that
both the Still Asking method and this comparison method are finished before the essay
explaining the site is published, Still Asking first ("Both, in that order"; decided by: mixed).
The Still Asking method was ruled the same day
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-21-still-asking-method-choices.md`).

## Choice 1. Who reads first

**Put to him.** John reads first, by hand, under a reading sheet written and committed before he
opens any answer (option A). He was told the size (100 replies so far, about 12,000 to 44,000
words; perhaps six and a half times as many if the OpenAI run is made in full), that his reading
carries his own published positions, and the other options: a person other than John; two
people each alone; a model from a different developer; a model run on this Mac; a script only;
and nobody. *Confidence:* moderate to high, because it is the only option that keeps every ruled
rule and fits the note. *Judgment call.* *Strongest alternative:* nobody reads for now, and the
answers are published whole when John decides, at the cost of leaving the comparison to
strangers. John: "Yes". **Decided by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.** John reads the capture's answers first, by hand, under a reading sheet
committed before he opens any. It closes open question 7 in part, for the capture's answers
only: who reads them first.

## Choice 2. When the first reading happens, and what follows it

**Put to him.** Not before this method is ruled, Still Asking's questions are written and
committed, and the OpenAI run is made or its last safe day (2026-12-09) has passed. The new
wording for the Still Asking page's sentence "They have not been read" goes to John before the
reading, as a dated correction of fact. After the reading, any change to Still Asking's questions
says it does not come from the capture's answers. He was told the cost (the two models already
asked may go unread for months) and that the essay does not wait for the reading itself, only
for the method. *Confidence:* high on Still Asking's questions first; moderate on waiting for
OpenAI. *Standard practice* for fixing a method before the evidence; *judgment call* on the
OpenAI wait. *Strongest alternative:* read the two models already asked as soon as this method is
ruled. John: "Yes, as proposed". **Decided by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.** Choice 2 as proposed.

## Choice 3. Whether a model may help read

**Put to him.** No model reads the unpublished answers, as first reader or as checker of John's
descriptions; the rule "never given to any model to sort or summarise" stays as ruled. Once the
answers are published whole, a different session may read them to check John's descriptions
before those are published, on the proposal's reading that checking a description against
public text is not sorting or summarising, which he was asked to confirm. He was told a model
wrote the recommendation. *Confidence:* moderate. *Judgment call.* *Strongest alternative:* a
model from a developer with no model in the capture, run on this Mac, used only after his
reading to check his descriptions, at the cost of revising a ruled rule. John: "Yes, as
proposed". **Decided by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.** Choice 3 as proposed, including the reading that checking a description
against published text is not sorting or summarising. It closes open question 7 in part, for the
capture's answers: no model reads them while unpublished.

## Choice 4. A second reader

**Put to him.** None for now. If John wants one, or wants descriptions checked before the answers
are public, a person, reading alone and writing descriptions before seeing his (option C). He
was told that two independent readers being common practice is from memory and unchecked.
*Confidence:* moderate. *Standard practice* for two readers; *judgment call* not to have one.
*Strongest alternative:* a second person from the start, at the cost of a spend and a stranger
reading the answers before they are published. John: "Yes, as proposed". **Decided by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.** Choice 4 as proposed. Any second reader before publication is a person.

## Choice 5. Hiding the models' names while reading

**Put to him.** A script hides each model's name behind a letter, shuffles the models' order
within each question, and keeps the key apart, outside every repository, until John's
descriptions for that question are written. He was told the hiding is partial and that its
being common practice is from memory and unchecked. *Confidence:* low to moderate. *Judgment
call.* *Strongest alternative:* read with names shown. John: "Yes, as proposed". **Decided by:
mixed.**

**What is ruled.** Choice 5 as proposed.

## Choice 6. What "counted" allows

**Put to him.** "Counted" rules out any tally, share, percentage or total built from the
answers, in a description, on a page, or across models. A description may point to the askings
a point rests on by number ("askings 1, 3 and 4") but writes no tally ("three of five"). The
shared-wording script's output is kept outside every repository and never published as a figure.
*Confidence:* moderate. *Judgment call*, a reading of a ruled word and not a change to it.
*Strongest alternative:* "counted" means counted into a figure, so a sentence may say "in three
of the five askings". John: "Yes, as proposed". **Decided by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.** Choice 6 as proposed.

## Choice 7. The reading sheet, for the present answers

**Put to him.** Two kinds of comparison, within one model across its five askings and across
models for one question, each described and never explained. A reading sheet with those boxes,
plus notes, committed before anyone reads, in the form choice 6 allows. A shared-wording script
run once on the present answers before John reads, its output kept outside every repository.
*Confidence:* high. *Standard practice* for fixing the analysis before the data; *judgment call*
on the boxes. *Strongest alternative:* no fixed sheet. John: "Yes, as proposed". **Decided by:
mixed.**

**What is ruled.** Choice 7 as proposed.

## Choice 8. Terms for later models, if a later asking is ever ruled

**Put to him.** No later asking is planned, and a yes plans none. If one is ever ruled, it is
compared on the terms in Part 2: the same bytes and settings, five askings, the three dates (the
developer's list published 2026-11-09, the questions public 2026-11-19, the answers' publication
if any) and the model's stated training cut-off beside every comparison, question 10 read first,
a shared-wording script before reading, the caution about what a later model may have read on
every question, and no difference written as a change in the models. *Confidence:* moderate.
*Standard practice* for fixing terms before the evidence; *judgment call* on the steps.
*Strongest alternative:* leave the terms unwritten until a later asking is proposed. John: "Yes,
as proposed". **Decided by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.** Choice 8 as proposed. No later asking is planned by it.

## Choice 9. The capture, the Still Asking card and the List's headline

**Put to him.** No capture answer carries a mark of any kind, not even "not assessed"; no
capture answer or description of one is cited on a mark sheet, whether or not published; no card
is shown beside the capture's answers; the capture's answers are never a baseline for Still
Asking; and nothing from the capture feeds the List's headline, its scale of what is offered
against what is known, or the list's growth. *Confidence:* high. *Standard practice* for keeping
a record apart; *judgment call* on the published-source rule. *Strongest alternative:* show the
line on training's general rule once on the capture's page. John: "Yes, as proposed". **Decided
by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.** Choice 9 as proposed.
