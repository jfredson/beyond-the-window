# Ruling: the Still Asking page says that the capture happened

Ruled by John on 2026-11-19, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session wrote the paragraph and recommended it, John approved). Written by Claude
(Fable 5.2), an AI model.

## How it came up

The Anthropic run of the sealed capture finished on 2026-11-19: ten questions put to Claude
Sonnet 4.5, five times each, 50 replies saved unread (`docs/spending-record.md`,
`docs/capture/manifest.md`). John's ruling of the same day
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-19-no-public-mention-of-ai-help-in-building.md`) says the one thing the
site discloses about AI is when a model has been asked the questions that make up the data. The
capture's replies are not part of the record, but a model was asked, so the session proposed
that the Still Asking page say so.

## What the session put to him

The paragraph, as shown to him:

> **One thing has been asked, outside this record.** On November 19th, 2026, ten questions were
> put to Claude Sonnet 4.5, five times each, shortly before its developer, Anthropic, withdrew
> it, so that its answers would exist after it was gone. No way of comparing answers had been
> written, so they are not part of Still Asking. The answers are kept exactly as given. They
> have not been read, they are not scored, and nothing on this site rests on them.

Below it the session added one wording point: "withdrew it" is in the past tense, the
withdrawal is on 2026-11-30, and until then the words should be "withdraws it on November 30th,
2026". The session said: "I would publish it that way now."

The question put to him was "Does the paragraph about the capture go on the Still Asking page
as written?" John: "Yes, as written".

## What is ruled

1. **The paragraph goes on the Still Asking page.**
2. **Until 2026-11-30 it reads "withdraws it on November 30th, 2026"** in place of "withdrew
   it". This is the session's reading of his answer, and is marked as that: he answered after
   the tense point was put to him, and the past tense would state something not yet true. If
   he meant the past tense, he says so and it is changed.

## What follows

- A session adds the paragraph, a different session checks it, and the master session pushes
  it, which publishes it.
- On or after 2026-11-30 the tense is changed to "withdrew it", once the withdrawal has been
  confirmed on Anthropic's own page.
- The two names in the paragraph are linked at first mention, as the workspace rule on links
  says.

## Not covered

- Reading the replies, which still waits for a written method, a check by a different session
  and John's ruling.
- The Google and OpenAI runs. If one happens, the paragraph is revised and put to John again.
