# Ruling: the Still Asking page says that Gemini 2.5 Flash was asked too

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session wrote the paragraph and recommended it, John approved). Written by Claude
(Fable 5.2), an AI model.

## How it came up

John's ruling of 2026-11-19 is that public pages say plainly when a model has been asked the
questions that make up the data
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-19-no-public-mention-of-ai-help-in-building.md`). The Still Asking page
carries one paragraph on the sealed capture, approved by him on 2026-11-19 and mended the same
day (`docs/rulings/2026-11-19-capture-paragraph-on-still-asking.md`,
`docs/rulings/2026-11-19-two-wording-fixes-on-still-asking.md`). It names Claude Sonnet 4.5
alone. On 2026-11-20 the Google run was made: Gemini 2.5 Flash answered, and Google would not
serve Gemini 2.5 Pro (`docs/spending-record.md`). So the page was out of date.

## The paragraph put to him

> **Two models have been asked, outside this record.** On November 19th, 2026, ten questions
> were put to Claude Sonnet 4.5, five times each, shortly before its developer, Anthropic, is
> due to withdraw it from its own services on November 30th, 2026, so that its answers would
> exist after it was gone. On November 20th, 2026, the same ten questions were put to Gemini
> 2.5 Flash, five times each, before its developer, Google, is due to withdraw it on December
> 1st, 2026. Google would not let this project ask Gemini 2.5 Pro, which it is withdrawing the
> same day. No way of comparing answers had been written, so they are not part of Still
> Asking. The answers are kept exactly as given. They have not been read, they are not scored,
> and nothing on this site rests on them.

What the session told him: the changes from the approved paragraph are the opening words and
the two sentences about Google. *Confidence:* high that the page must change, moderate on the
sentence about Gemini 2.5 Pro, kept because a reader who knows Google is withdrawing two models
would otherwise wonder about the second. *Standard practice or judgment call:* it follows the
paragraph he approved on 2026-11-19, in the same form. *Strongest alternative:* leave Gemini
2.5 Pro out, which is shorter and says nothing about a model that was not asked.

The question put to him was "Say on the Still Asking page that Gemini 2.5 Flash was asked too,
in the wording shown?" John: "Yes, as written". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The paragraph above replaces the one on the Still Asking page, word for word. Each model and
developer named gets its link, opened first. A writing session changes the page, a different
session checks it, and the master session pushes.

## Not covered by this ruling

Publishing or reading any answer, and any other sentence on the site.
