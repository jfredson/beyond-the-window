## How it came up

This is the last of the seven points John decides before a session writes the third caution
into the records (`docs/research/2026-11-18-third-caution-sources.md`, section 6). The research
session made one pass looking for sources. The session that checked it found eight sources the

## What the session told him

- *The choice:* build the entries now and search once more before release, or hold every entry
  until the search is finished.
- *The recommendation:* build now. Entries with a source that has been opened and checked get
  their caution written. The rest say nothing is recorded yet. Before anything is released, one
  more bounded pass is made.
- *Why:* nothing is published, and an entry can be added to.
- *Confidence:* moderate. That the first pass missed three statements in papers already cited
  is the best evidence that more is there, so the second pass is not optional.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* judgment call.
- *Strongest alternative:* hold every entry until the search is finished. Its cost is delay,
  and its gain is that no entry is ever written twice.
- *Three things he was told before answering:* the second pass is limited to three tasks, with
  anything beyond them going in a list for John and not into the entries; it needs no
  spending, and papers behind a paywall stay unread and are listed as such; and the second
  pass is recorded as a condition of release.

The question put to him was "Build the entries now and search once more before release?" John:
"Yes, build now". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

1. **The entries are built now.** An entry with a source that has been opened and checked gets
   its caution written. An entry without one says nothing is recorded yet.
2. **One more bounded search pass is made before anything is released.** It has three tasks:
   - read in full every paper an entry cites by summary only;
   - try again the papers that would not open;
   - read the relevant chapters of the books.
3. **Release waits for that pass.** Nothing on the site is published until it has been made and
   a different session has checked it.

## How the pass is bounded (as the session told John; decided by: mixed)

- It does the three tasks and no open-ended searching.
- Anything it turns up beyond them goes in a list for John and not into the entries.
- It spends nothing. A paper behind a paywall stays unread and is listed as unread.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **Publishing.** This ruling adds a condition of release. It is not a go to release, and
  whether, when and where the site is published are still John's.
