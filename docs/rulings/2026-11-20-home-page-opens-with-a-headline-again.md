# Ruling: the home page opens with a headline number again, as the sibling sites do

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (John asked, the session recommended, John directed). Written by Claude (Opus 5.5), an
AI model, in the master planning session (which wrote with Fable 5.2 until that point the same
day).

## How it came up

John asked: "Did we lose the "headline number" concept for the homepage of the site that we are
using across all the other tracker sites? The site right now seems to just feature a headline
from a recent news article, but that shouldn't be the main headline for new readers visiting
the site. What is the main headline that we previously discussed for the project?"

The session answered that the rebuilt home page, published on 2026-11-20 by another planning
session on John's go ("Pursue the rebuild"; then "Yes, publish it", given before he had seen
the page; `docs/rulings/2026-11-20-pursue-the-rebuild.md`,
`docs/rulings/2026-11-20-go-to-publish-the-rebuilt-home-page-once-checked.md`), had set aside
the rulings that the home page opens with the count
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-18-home-page-count.md`) and the sentence beside it
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-19-home-page-sentence.md`), and no longer showed the founding question.
The new top line was one dated act. The session said it had seen the rebuild go out and had
not told him the count had left the home page, which it should have.

Asked why, the session set out the cold-reader audit
(`docs/proposals/2026-11-20-what-a-stranger-makes-of-the-site.md`): two AI readers per
version, on one phone screen, could not say what the count counted; the caveat under it read
as the site disowning it; four different counts showed in two screens; and nothing gave a
reason to care. Its view: the test showed the number was badly explained, not that a headline
number cannot work here, and the gains of the rebuild (the subject in plain words, the
developer named, the readings) could stay beneath a restored number.

## His words

John: "Yes please rebuild it with something as a headline just like we have for the other
tracker sites".

## What is ruled

The home page opens, as the sibling sites do, with a headline number: a short line saying what
the site is, the number, one plain sentence saying what is counted, and the question. The
rebuild's readings may stay beneath it.

## Not ruled yet

- **The exact number and sentence.** The ruled sentence of 2026-11-19 and a plainer one are to
  be drawn as alternatives, and the count must be worked out from the records, never typed in.
- **The layout,** which is built as a draft and shown to John as pictures before anything is
  published. Nothing reaches the live site until he has seen it.
- **The fraction.** The Upgrade Tree shows "28 of 35"; this site's ruling of 2026-11-18 is a
  count with no total beside it. That ruling stands unless John changes it.
