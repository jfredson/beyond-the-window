# Ruling: what "offered today" means, and memory across conversations sits beside the list

Ruled by John on 2026-11-18, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## What was put to him

After his ruling that the home page opens with a count and no fraction
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-18-home-page-count.md`), a session changed the sentence at the top to
say six things "are offered today by at least one developer of AI models". A different session
checked the change (`docs/reviews/2026-11-18-check-of-count-and-authorship.md`, finding 1) and
found that the list page and the About page still print the older meaning of the word:
"offered" means made available to a model, and a promise is not counted. Two of the six counted
records do not fit that meaning. Keeping the files of retired models is shown as "Promised in
public", and a welfare assessment at release is published to the public and not given to a
model. The check said the meaning of "offered today" decides what is counted, so it is John's
to rule, and suggested this wording:

> "Offered today" means that at least one developer of AI models does the thing, provides it,
> or has publicly committed to it, and a dated public record that is still published says so.
> A plan, a research result, or a promise that has not yet taken effect is not counted.

The planning session recommended adopting it. *Confidence:* moderate to high. It matches what
the records already count. *Strongest alternative:* keep the narrow meaning and stop counting
the records that do not fit, which makes the count smaller and leaves out a commitment that is
in force today.

He was also asked, for the second time that day, about the record for memory across
conversations. Its own text says the memory is about the user, is the user's to view and
delete, and that placing it on the list "may be wrong in kind". The planning session told him
the wider meaning makes this a closer call, since memory is something a developer provides, and
still recommended moving it beside the list, because the list is about what an AI model might
reach for and this memory is kept for the user. *Confidence:* moderate. *Strongest
alternative:* keep it counted with the doubt stated on its page, since memory is what many
readers would expect to find on the list.

He was offered "Yes to both" or "Keep memory counted".

John: "Yes to both". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

1. **"Offered today" has the wider meaning quoted above.** A developer doing the thing,
   providing it, or having publicly committed to it all count, where a dated public record that
   is still published says so. A plan, a research result, or a promise that has not yet taken
   effect does not.
2. **The record for memory across conversations moves beside the list** and is no longer
   counted. The record is kept, with its sources and its text, and its page says why it sits
   where it does.

By the records as they stand, the count becomes 5. It is worked out from the records by the
script, as always, and never typed in.

## What is not ruled

- **The exact words of the definition on the page, and of the sentence at the top.** The
  definition above is the meaning. A session fits the words to the page, a different session
  checks them, and John sees them in place.
- **Whether any other record should move** on or off the list under the wider meaning. None
  was put to him.
- Whether a kind of memory kept for the model itself, if a developer ever offers one, belongs
  on the list. That would be a new record.

Nothing is published or deployed.
