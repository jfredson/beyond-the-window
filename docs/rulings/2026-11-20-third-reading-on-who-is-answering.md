# Ruling: a third reading, on who is answering, with the five conditions held until their sources are read

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

This is the second of six changes in the proposal
`docs/proposals/2026-11-17-how-a-machines-word-earns-weight.md`. The first, ruled the same day
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-20-first-rule-in-five-parts.md`), says the weight given to a machine's
word rises as it meets conditions fixed in advance. This change says what the conditions are
and where they are shown.

## What was put to him

- *The proposal:* a third reading beside the two every entry has. It is on the speaker and not
  on the entry. Every answer in Still Asking would carry a short card saying which of five
  conditions the machine that gave it meets: a kept past; mattering; a point of view; checked
  self-reports; wants that hold. Each is marked met, partly met, not met or not measured, with
  its source, tested by something other than the machine's word, and there is no single score.
- *The recommendation:* adopt the third reading, and hold the five until their sources are
  read. The proposal says the five were drawn from summaries of John's published positions and
  that the full sources were not read, and lists that as a way it could be wrong.
- *Confidence:* high that the card is the right shape, moderate on these five as the five.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* a judgment call.
- *Strongest alternative:* adopt the five now, so the revised rule has its conditions at once,
  at the cost that a later full reading may change one after it was fixed.
- *The overlap:* two of the conditions are also things the site follows as aspirations, so
  progress on those would also raise the weight of answers, and the site should say so.

The question put to him was "Adopt a third reading on who is answering, with the five
conditions held until their sources are read?" John: "Yes, sources first". **Decided by:
mixed.**

## What is ruled

1. **There is a third reading, on who is answering,** shown as a card on an answer in Still
   Asking, with no single score.
2. **The five conditions are not fixed.** A session reads each condition's source in full and
   reports whether the five survive. The result goes to John, and he rules on the conditions
   then.

## Not covered by this ruling

- The conditions themselves, the tests for each, and the wording of the card.
- Any public page, and any answer already kept. Until the conditions are ruled, part (e) of
  the first rule raises no machine's weight.
- The proposal's other four changes.
