# Ruling: on the About page, the Warsaw paper's main claim comes after its concession

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an

## How it came up

The read of the live pages against John's published positions
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-20-live-pages-read-against-the-positions.md`, its sixth lean) found that
the About page describes two papers arguing opposite ways, and that both descriptions end on
the risk of crediting an AI system with too little. For the second paper, by Emilia Kaczmarek,
the last sentence is her concession against her own argument.

## What was put to him

The two sentences now on the page:

> ... that crediting AI with too much moral standing is the nearer danger, because its harms
> are already happening. The same paper grants that crediting it with too little "could carry
> a higher potential for harm".

Proposed, with nothing added or removed beyond the joins:

> The paper grants that crediting AI with too little "could carry a higher potential for
> harm". Its argument is that crediting it with too much moral standing is the nearer danger,
> because its harms are already happening.

- *Confidence:* moderate. Both statements were already on the page and checked against the
  paper, and the checking session that read the paper in full on 2026-11-20 called it the
  first caution's own case, argued in full.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* a judgment call.
- *Strongest alternative:* leave the order, which may follow each paper's own, the substance
  having been settled on 2026-11-19.

The question put to him was "Reorder the two sentences about the Warsaw paper on the About
page, so its main claim comes last?" John: "Yes, reorder". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The description of that paper on the About page ends with her argument, in the proposed
wording, fitted to the sentence that names her and the paper without changing what it says.
