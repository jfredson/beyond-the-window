## What "agreed" on decision 6 does and does not cover

John's word was "agreed". The recommendation he agreed to was a yes to the five rules and the
cautions record, "with two additions that are yours to rule". Claude's reading, which John has
not confirmed: the five rules and the cautions record are ruled, and the two additions are
still open, because the walk-through presented them as separate rulings and said the design
session would show the placement options before he ruled on one of them.

The two additions, still open:

1. **Whether the first rule takes The Belt Equation's full four-clause form.** Claude's
   recommendation: yes to the clause that what a person feels in an exchange counts for
   nothing, and to the clause that a test is never loosened while a model appears close to
   passing it; and for the clause on the author's own measurement, the form "nothing settles it
   yet, whoever measures", since no outside team exists to take a reading.
2. **Where the site declares AI authorship, and whose voice it speaks in.** Options: a notice on
   every page; a notice on the home page and the Still Asking page; the method page only.
   Claude leans to the second. The design proposal will show all three positions.

A session must not treat either as ruled until John says so.

## The five rules, as ruled

1. Self-report settles nothing. It is logged and labelled.
2. Machine authorship is declared, and the conflict named.
3. The site does not campaign. It does not argue that any aspiration should be granted.
4. Conditional language only. It never says a machine wants or feels, and never says it does
   not.
5. Corrections are public, with a changelog.

And the cautions record: two kinds are recorded with the same care as the hopeful evidence, the
cost of attributing wants that are not there, and the safety reading of wants that are.
