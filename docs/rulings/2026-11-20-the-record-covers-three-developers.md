# Ruling: the record covers the three developers it names now

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with a working session (not the master planning
session). **Decided by: mixed** (a session proposed, John chose). Written by Claude
(Fable 5.2), an AI model.

## How it came up

John ruled the same day that the rebuilt home page names every developer the record covers
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-20-home-page-names-every-covered-developer.md`). That left open who they
are (decision 2 of `docs/proposals/2026-11-20-rebuild-design.md`).

## What was put to him

- **The three named now:** Anthropic, OpenAI and Google, the developers already listed in
  `data/developers.toml`. Quick to start, and "1 of 3" invites the question of why three.
- **Find a wider list.** More defensible. The session said it knew of no outside list of
  leading developers that would settle the matter without a judgment, so one would have to be
  looked for first.

*The session's proposal:* a stated rule and a named list on the About page, starting with the
three and adding others by ruling. *Confidence:* high that a stated list is needed; low on
which list. *Standard practice or judgment call:* judgment call. *Strongest alternative:* no
denominator at all, with "none found elsewhere" stated with the date and scope of the search.

A conflict was declared: one of the three, Anthropic, made the model doing the work, and it is
the only one for which the records hold anything offered.

Asked "Which developers should Beyond the Window's record cover?", John answered: "The three
named now". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The record covers three developers: Anthropic, OpenAI and Google. They are the columns of the
developers' reading on the rebuilt home page.

## What follows

- The page says in plain words that the record covers these three and that others are not
  covered yet, so that nothing found among three is not read as nothing found anywhere.
- A developer is added by John's ruling.
- Each of the three is searched for each thing on the list before a cell says nothing was
  found. Those searches need John's go, as any reading for the record does.

## What is not ruled

- **The rule for adding a fourth.** No rule is stated; a developer is added when John says so.
- **The wording** of the sentence that says who is covered.
- **The published site,** which does not change until John approves a rebuilt page.
