# Ruling: the home page headline shows offered against known, choice by choice

Ruled by John from 2026-11-21, in conversation with the master planning session. The choices are
those in `docs/proposals/2026-11-21-headline-fraction.md` on branch `claude/headline-fraction-cf25be`,
drafted by a writing session and found fit to put to John by a different session
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-21-check-of-the-headline-fraction.md`). Written by Claude (Opus 5.5), an AI
model.

## How it came up

John on 2026-11-21: "Simply showing a 5 isn't very compelling. Show 5 out of 8 or 5 out of 85 is
more interesting. Having the total number of identified near-term things that a mind might
verifiably reach for is just as important as counting the ones that are enabled by some developer
today. And over time that number will change just as the available-today number will change."
Earlier the same day he had agreed that a scale of what is offered against what is known belongs
on the List's headline, with the growth of the known shown, and not on the Still Asking card. He
approved drafting it ("Yes, draft it").

## Choice 1. Replace the ruling of 2026-11-18, a count with no total, with "5 of 8"

**Put to him**, with pictures at phone and computer width. The home page shows the count and the
number of things on the List, both worked out from the records. *Confidence:* moderate to high,
because the reason the 2026-11-18 ruling gave (a total reads as progress toward a known end) is met
by saying beside the number that the 8 is what has been found so far and changes. *Standard
practice* in the family (The Upgrade Tree's "28 of 35"). A reading risk the check raised was named:
"5 of 8 done" invites "3 to go". *Strongest alternative:* keep the count alone and give the 8 in a
sentence under it. John: "Yes, 5 of 8". **Decided by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.** The home page headline shows the count against the number of things on the List,
both computed from the records. This replaces `docs/rulings/2026-11-18-home-page-count.md` on the
point of a total, which is kept on record. The older texts that describe the count as having no
total are brought into line when the branch is merged.

## Choice 2. How the fraction is shown

**Put to him.** The Upgrade Tree's form: the count in the largest type, then "of 8" smaller and in a
quieter colour on the same line, with a space before "of" inside the smaller type so a screen
reader or copied text says "5 of 8". *Confidence:* high. *Standard practice.* *Strongest
alternative:* "5 / 8", or the 8 on a line of its own. He asked what the choice meant and was told
plainly. John: "Yes". **Decided by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.** Choice 2 as proposed.
