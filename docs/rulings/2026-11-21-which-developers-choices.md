# Ruling: which developers the record covers, choice by choice

Ruled by John from 2026-11-21, in conversation with the master planning session. The choices are
those in `docs/proposals/2026-11-21-which-developers-the-record-covers.md`, Part 5, drafted by a
writing session and found fit to put to John by a different session
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-21-check-of-which-developers-the-record-covers.md`). Written by Claude
(Opus 5.5), an AI model.

## How it came up

John asked "Are we being shortsighted by only counting Anthropic, OpenAI and Google?", approved
drafting a rule a reader can check, and then ruled that this is settled, the searches made and
checked, before the essay introducing the site is published ("I want to go through this process
before publishing the essay, let's start").

## Choice 1. Widen the record by a rule, or keep the three and say why

**Put to him.** Cover the developers a stated, checkable rule gives, read on a stated date. Under
the proposal's recommended rule (top five of at least two of three named rankings) that adds Meta
and Moonshot; under the top ten of all three it adds Alibaba, DeepSeek, xAI and Z.ai as well.
*Confidence:* moderate. *Judgment call.* *Strongest alternative:* keep the three and say why on the
About page. Before answering John asked "How does Grok and XAI not make the list?" and was shown
xAI's places (10th, 7th and 7th on the three rankings, in each top ten and no top five), the
conflicting Artificial Analysis figure (46 in a post, 35 on the leaderboard the same day), and that
capability is not the only reason to cover a developer. John: "Yes, widen the rule". **Decided by:
mixed.**

**What is ruled.** The record covers the developers a stated, checkable rule gives, read on a stated
date. This replaces `docs/rulings/2026-11-20-the-record-covers-three-developers.md` on who is covered,
once the rule is chosen. The three stay covered in any case.

## Choice 2. Which rule

**Put to him.** Three forms: the top five of at least two of the three named rankings (the
proposal's recommendation, five developers); the top ten of at least two of the three (nine
developers, the master session's recommendation, worded "at least two" rather than the proposal's
"all three", which gives the same nine today and holds better if one ranking moves); or the clear
gap after third place (the present three). The rankings are Arena's chat ranking by lab, Artificial
Analysis's intelligence index and Epoch AI's capabilities index, read on 2026-11-21. *Confidence:*
moderate. *Standard practice* to follow an outside list; *judgment call* on the line at ten.
*Strongest alternative:* the top five, at a third of the searches. John reviewed the nine by name
and asked for a sourced account of the four Chinese developers before deciding: why they matter to
the site's question, how far the rule is mechanical, and their documented ties to the Chinese state.
After reading it, John: "Yes, all nine". **Decided by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.** The record covers every developer whose best model is in the top ten of at least
two of the three rankings named above, read on a stated date, with a saved copy of each ranking.
Read on 2026-11-21 it gives nine: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, xAI, Moonshot, Alibaba, DeepSeek
and Zhipu (Z.ai). The four Chinese developers are included because the rule gives them, and on the
same terms as the rest. Covering a developer means reading its public pages and news about it;
no model of any covered developer is used, run or asked anything under this ruling.
