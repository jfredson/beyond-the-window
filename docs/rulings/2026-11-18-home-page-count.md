# Ruling: the home page opens with a count and no fraction

Ruled by John on 2026-11-18, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John chose). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an AI
model.

## What was put to him

The first version of the site opens with a large "6 of 9" and the words "things on the list are
offered to at least one AI model today". A session chose that form on 2026-11-17 so that there
would be something to look at (`docs/first-version-choices.md`, choice 3). It was never ruled.
The check of the site's text (`docs/reviews/2026-11-17-site-text-against-positions.md`) flagged
it as an unruled question shown as settled.

The session gave him two faults in the number. The list holds only things a dated source could
be found for, so "of 9" reads as progress toward a known total when nobody knows the total. And
one of the six, memory across conversations, may not belong, by its own record's account.

He was shown three choices: a count with no fraction; no number yet, with the question as the
headline and the date the world's record was last read; or "6 of 9" kept and marked as a first
count. The session recommended the count with no fraction. *Confidence:* moderate, because it
had not been seen on the page. *Standard practice or judgment call:* judgment call; the nearest
sibling practice is The Upgrade Tree, which shows a count and refuses to guess. *Strongest
alternative:* no number yet, which claims least and leaves a reader nothing to repeat.

John: "Count, no fraction". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The home page opens with a count of the things on the list that are offered today, worked out
from the records and never typed in, with no total beside it. The "of 9" goes, on the home page
and wherever else the same sentence is used.

This rules the form of what stands at the top, which is part of open question 4. It is recorded
there.

## What is not ruled

- **The exact sentence.** It is drafted by a session, checked by a different one, and shown to
  John on the page before it stands.
- **Whether memory across conversations is counted or moved beside the list.** The session's
  view, given to John and not ruled, is to move it beside the list, which would make the count
  five. Until he rules, the record stays where it is and the count stays six.
- **Anything about the second half of the question**, what an AI model says or chooses. The
  count is of the world's record alone. Whether that half is ever summarised at the top stays
  open under question 4.
- The other points of the design proposal, and the two other decisions the site's release waits
  on: where the site says an AI model drafted much of it, and the missing caution.

Nothing is published or deployed.
