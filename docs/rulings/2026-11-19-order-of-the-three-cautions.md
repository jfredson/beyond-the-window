# Ruling: the three cautions are shown in the order of their headings

Ruled by John on 2026-11-19, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

The ruling on the headings (`docs/rulings/2026-11-19-caution-headings.md`) left the order on an
entry page unruled. Until 2026-11-19 the page showed the risk caution first. The session that
wrote the third caution into the entries changed the order to match the headings ruling and
marked the change PROPOSED (`docs/reviews/2026-11-19-third-caution-written.md`, section 5,
item 1).

## What the session told him

- *The choice:* the order of the headings ruling, or the risk caution first as before.
- *The recommendation:* the order of the headings ruling.
- *Why:* the first two are mistakes in opposite directions, and side by side at the start the
  pair is visible at a glance. The third is a risk and not a mistake, and reads naturally
  after them.
- *Confidence:* moderate. The master session had not seen the built page, and the writing
  session reports that the three columns are of very unequal length on some entries.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* judgment call.
- *Strongest alternative:* risk first, so that a reader who arrives worried meets the worry
  first. Its cost is that the mirrored pair is split from the start of the row.

The question put to him was "In what order are the three cautions shown on an entry page?",
with the first answer offered being "Your headings' order". John: "Your headings' order".
**Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

On every entry page the cautions are shown in this order:

1. If an AI system has no wants, and is treated as if it had
2. If an AI system has wants, and they go unnoticed
3. If an AI system has wants, and they put people at risk

The PROPOSED mark on the order comes off.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **The layout,** three columns on a wide screen, which is still PROPOSED and which John sees
  on the page.

Nothing is published.
