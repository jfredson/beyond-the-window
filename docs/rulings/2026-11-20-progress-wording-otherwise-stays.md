# Ruling: the rest of the site's "how far" wording stays as it is

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session recommended leaving it, John agreed). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an AI
model.

## How it came up

The read of the live pages against John's published positions
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-20-live-pages-read-against-the-positions.md`, its first lean) found that
the site describes each thing as a step along a road: "beyond", "limits" and "how far off" in
the name and the question, "Not offered yet" and "how far the world has got on each" on the
home page, and the word "offered". John's positions argue that new kinds of mind are worth
wanting, on conditions, so the frame's words and his case point the same way. The labels on
entry pages lost the word "progress" the same day
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-20-progress-dropped-from-the-kind-labels.md`). The reader suggested also
changing "Not offered yet" to "Not offered" and "how far the world has got on each" to "where
each thing stands".

## What was put to him

The session recommended leaving the rest: the name, the question and "offered" are John's and
ruled; the two phrases follow from them, and changing them alone would make the site
inconsistent without making it neutral; the sibling sites use the same words; and the About
page already says the wording supposes and does not claim. *Confidence:* moderate. *Strongest
alternative:* change the two phrases, which is cheap and claims a little less.

The question put to him was "Leave the rest of the site's 'how far' wording as it is?" John:
"Yes, leave it". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

"Not offered yet", "how far the world has got on each", the name, the question and the word
"offered" stay. A later read against the positions need not raise them again.

## Not covered by this ruling

Whether the site's frame itself should be different, which the session said is a question
about the premise and would bring separately if John wants it looked at.
