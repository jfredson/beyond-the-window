# Ruling on the design direction

Ruled by John on 2026-11-17. Asked which direction the site's look should take, Glass or Page,
after seeing the eleven mock-up pictures of `docs/design/2026-11-17-design-proposal.md`, he
answered: "Glass". **Decided by: mixed** (a design session proposed both directions; John
chose).

## What is ruled

Point 1 of the eight the design proposal asks him to rule: **the direction is Glass**, the
window-based look, and not Page, the typed-sheet alternative.

## What is not ruled

His answer was to the question of direction only. The proposal's other seven points stay open:

2. The central picture: the window as drawn, or another.
3. What stands at the top of the page (open question 4).
4. Where the site says an AI model wrote much of it (open question 7).
5. The default theme.
6. Whether the two meaning-colours are kept (amber for what the world's record shows, dusk blue
   for what AI models said when asked).
7. The top bar's five pages, and whether Cautions is one of them.
8. The skin's name. "Glass" was his word for the direction; whether the skin is named Glass,
   Sill or something else has not been asked separately.

A session must not treat any of these as ruled.

## Two things noted when the pictures were relayed

- The mock-ups were drawn before the proposal on how a machine's word earns weight
  (`docs/proposals/2026-11-17-how-a-machines-word-earns-weight.md`). The entry page leads the
  machine side with what models said, and shows no card for who is answering. If that proposal
  is ruled, the entry page needs redrawing to its order of evidence.
- The headline form that counts matches with human wants ("6 · 4 · 3" in the mock-up) counts
  what the Sentient Horizons positions call the least informative result.

Nothing is built. The Page mock-ups stay in `docs/design/` as the record of the alternative.
