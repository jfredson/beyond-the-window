# Ruling: the word "progress" is dropped from the three labels on entry pages

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## How it came up

The one-time read of the live pages against John's published positions
(`docs/reviews/2026-11-20-live-pages-read-against-the-positions.md`) found that the site's
frame uses the vocabulary of progress. Its plainest case: every entry page carries one of the
labels "Technical progress", "Political progress" or "Cultural progress", and the entry on
laws against legal personhood for AI systems is labelled "Political progress". The session
checked that the labels are still on the entry pages after the rebuilt home page went out.

## What was put to him

- *The problem:* a reader takes "Political progress" on that entry either as the site calling
  the bans progress, or as a mistake. And "progress" on every entry says that movement toward
  these things is the good direction, on a site that says it recommends nothing.
- *The fix:* drop the word from all three, so the labels read "Technical", "Political" and
  "Cultural". Nothing else on the page changes.
- *Confidence:* high.
- *Standard practice or judgment call:* a judgment call. The session had not checked how the
  sibling sites label kinds of change.
- *Strongest alternative:* keep the labels, since "progress" is the working word in the method
  and the records and a bare "Political" tells a reader a little less.
- *What it does not settle:* the wider finding that the whole frame speaks of progress.

The question put to him was "Drop the word 'progress' from the three labels on entry pages?"
John: "Yes, drop it". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

On every public page where one of the three labels is shown, it reads "Technical", "Political"
or "Cultural", without the word "progress".

## Not covered by this ruling

- The records' own field (`progress_kind`) and the working documents, which keep their words.
- Any other wording on the site, and the wider finding about the frame.
