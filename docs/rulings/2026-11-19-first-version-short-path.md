# Ruling: the first version takes the short path, and the rest is improved in public

Ruled by John on 2026-11-19, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an AI model.

## How it came up

While the session was walking him through the site decisions that were left, John asked: "How
soon can we publish the first version of the site and then begin iterating from there?"

## What the session told him

The session's estimate, its own and resting on hosting it had not checked: a first version
could be ready for his go in two to three days, around 2026-11-21 or 2026-11-22.

**What must happen before the first version goes out:**

1. The third caution is written into the entries and checked.
2. The second search pass is made and checked, as ruled the same day
   (`docs/rulings/2026-11-19-build-now-search-again-before-release.md`).
3. One full check by a session that wrote none of the site: every link opened, every fact
   traced to an opened source, and the ten facts still written from memory either sourced or
   taken off the pages.
4. John reads the words that are in his own first person: the notice on the home page and the
   "Who does what" section of the About page.
5. Somewhere to put it. The address, beyondthewindow.org, was registered by John on
   2026-11-17. Making a repository on GitHub, pushing to it and setting up the host each need
   his word.
6. John's go to publish.

**What can wait and be improved in public:** the sentence beside the count (which he then
settled anyway); the figures under the window drawing and the other design points; whether the
entry on developers' published rules stays beside the list; sharing text and pictures.

**One thing that cannot wait:** the About page says "No AI model has been asked anything for
this site." Once the sealed capture runs, that is false. If the site is out before the run,
the sentence changes on the day of the run; if after, before release. The Still Asking page
must say plainly that ten questions were put to models about to be withdrawn, under no ruled
method, and that the answers feed nothing.

The session offered: "If you would rather take the short path and leave this sentence marked
as unsettled, say so and I will plan the work that way." John: "Shorter version, and take the
short path". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

1. **The work is planned along the short path:** the six things above come first, and the
   things listed as able to wait are taken up after the first version is out.
2. **The first version is improved in public after that,** with corrections dated and visible
   as guideline 7 says.

## Not covered by this ruling

- **This is not the go to publish.** Step 6 is still his, and so is each part of step 5.
- **It does not close open question 13 (timing).** His words point to publishing before the end
  of 2026, and the question closes when he gives the go or says otherwise.
- **It does not lift any rule about sources.** Nothing reaches a public page until its source
  has been opened.
- **The Still Asking page.** Whether it goes out in the first version was not put to him as a
  separate choice. The session's plan (decided by: agent) is that it goes out saying what has
  and has not been asked, and John sees the page before the go.

Nothing is published, and nothing has been pushed.
