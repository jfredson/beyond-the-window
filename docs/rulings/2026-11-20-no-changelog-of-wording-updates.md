# Ruling: no changelog of wording updates

Ruled by John on 2026-11-20, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
John**, against the session's recommendation. Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an AI model.

## How it came up

On 2026-11-20 a sentence on the entry about laws against legal personhood was reworded, because
it gave one sponsor's aim in words its source does not use
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-20-liability-cautions-refiled-on-the-bans-entry.md`). A checking session
said a dated sentence on the entry's page was owed, under guideline 7 ("Corrections are public.
Once there is anything public, every correction is dated and stays visible."). The master
session proposed such a sentence, recommended it, and asked "Add the dated correction sentence,
as shown, to the entry on laws against AI personhood?" It also said entry pages would need a
new place for a correction with its own words.

## His words

John: "I don't think we need to keep a changelog of wording updates".

## What is ruled

No dated sentence is added to that entry's page for the rewording, and no place for such
sentences is built into entry pages.

## How the session applies it, which is the session's reading and not his words

- **A change of wording gets no public note:** not on a page, and from now on not as a line in
  `CHANGELOG.md` either. The commit and, where John ruled the wording, the ruling file are the
  record.
- **Guideline 7 still holds for a correction of fact:** a wrong date, number, name, source or
  count on a public page is still dated and stays visible. John's sentence speaks of wording,
  and the session does not read it as lifting that.
- **The line between the two is a judgment.** The rewording that raised this made a sentence
  claim less than before, to match its source, and John calls it a wording update. So the
  session treats a sentence brought into line with its source as wording, and a changed fact
  as a correction. A case that is not clear goes to John.
- **Lines already in `CHANGELOG.md` stay as they are.**

If John meant something narrower or wider, this file is corrected.
