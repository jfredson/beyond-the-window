# Ruling: OpenAI's date in the developers' table is corrected to when its change was seen in effect

Ruled by John on 2026-11-21, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Opus 5.5), an
AI model.

## How it came up

OpenAI's place in the developers' table, for the entry on ending a conversation, reads "Since
October 30th, 2026". The drafting session for the restored headline found that this is the date
of OpenAI's notice, which said the change "will begin rolling out in the next few weeks". The
help page opened on 2026-11-20 says the interventions are "enabled for personal ChatGPT accounts
worldwide" and "gradually rolling out to accounts worldwide", with no date of its own. So the
row dates the change to its announcement, not to when it took effect, and no dated act records
it taking effect. This is a correction of fact, so under guideline 7 as narrowed on 2026-11-20
(`docs/rulings/2026-11-20-no-changelog-of-wording-updates.md`) it is dated and stays visible.

## What was put to him

1. Add a dated act, dated 2026-11-20, the day the help page was read: "says its chat product's
   safety interventions, which can end a conversation, are enabled for personal accounts
   worldwide and rolling out gradually". The act of 2026-10-30 stays, as the announcement.
2. Change the row's date to "By November 20th, 2026".
3. A dated correction note on the entry's page.

*Confidence:* high that the date is wrong; moderate on "By November 20th", since the real date
the change took effect is not known. *Strongest alternative:* keep October 30th with
"announced" beside it, which leaves a row in the in-place column dated to a day the change was
not in place.

The question, put in the message, was whether to make these three changes. John: "Yes, as
proposed". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

The three changes above. The count does not move.

## A second answer the same day: two wordings, and how the table shows "By"

The writing session stopped before changing anything. The table takes each developer's date from
that developer's first act on the entry, so adding the new act alone would leave "Since October
30th, 2026" in place. To show "By", the act of 2026-10-30 is marked as an announcement that the
table's date skips, the new act is marked as the change seen in effect with no known start date,
and the table writes "By" where that mark is set and "Since" elsewhere. That mechanism is the
master session's call. **Decided by: agent.**

Two wordings went to John. First, the help page says the interventions are enabled "for personal
ChatGPT accounts worldwide in Chat mode" and do not apply in business workspaces or voice mode,
so the ruled act words said a little more than the page. Second, the table's explanation covered
only "Since". The question was whether to approve both as shown. John: "Yes, both as shown".
**Decided by: mixed.**

**What is ruled.**

- The new act's words: "says its chat product's safety interventions, which can end a
  conversation, are enabled for personal accounts worldwide in its chat mode and rolling out
  gradually".
- A line in the table's explanation, beside the one for "Since": ""By" with a date means the
  thing was seen in effect on that day, and the day it began is not known."
