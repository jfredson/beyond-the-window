# Ruling: how the site says an AI model drafted much of it

Ruled by John on 2026-11-18, in conversation with the master planning session. **Decided by:
mixed** (a session proposed and recommended, John approved). Written by Claude (Fable 5.2), an
AI model.

## What was put to him

The first version of the site says an AI model drafted much of it in three places, put there by
a session on 2026-11-17 and never ruled (`docs/first-version-choices.md`, choice 5): a short
notice on the home page and the Still Asking page; a section on the About page that states the
conflict of interest; and one line on every entry page. No tool is named. The About page says
the drafting model is made by one of the developers whose commitments appear in the entries,
without saying which.

The session told him why it needed his ruling. The family rule is that tools are not
advertised, and that rule keeps disclosure where a reader needs it to judge the work. Here the
site's subject is AI models, so an AI model drafting it is a conflict of interest about
everything on the page.

He was shown three choices: keep the three placements; the About page only, as the family does
now; or a notice on every page. The session recommended keeping the three placements with two
changes:

1. Name the developer on the About page, in the conflict paragraph only: say plainly that the
   drafting model is made by Anthropic, whose commitments several entries count. The model's
   name stays out.
2. Put the notice and the About section in John's own first person ("I direct the sessions, an
   AI model drafted much of the text, and I decide what stands"). The records stay in a plain
   impersonal voice.

*Confidence:* high on declaring it in these places; moderate on naming the developer and on the
first person. *Standard practice or judgment call:* judgment call, following the family rule's
own exception and the notice Minimum Viable Mind carries. *Strongest alternative:* the About
page only, which keeps the family consistent and avoids putting readers off, at the cost of the
one site where the disclosure matters most saying it least.

John: "As recommended". **Decided by: mixed.**

## What is ruled

- The site says an AI model drafted much of it in three places: the notice on the home page and
  the Still Asking page, the About page's section on who does what, and a line on every entry
  page.
- The About page's conflict paragraph names Anthropic as the maker of the drafting model. No
  model is named, there or anywhere else on the site.
- The notice and the About section on who does what are written in John's first person. The
  records and the rest of the site stay in a plain impersonal voice.

This is an exception, for this site, to the family's practice that tools are not named in public
text. It does not change that practice for any other site.

## What is not ruled

- **The exact sentences.** John reads and owns every first-person sentence before release. A
  session drafts them, a different session checks them, and he sees them on the page.
- **Whether the entry pages say who made the second check of each record**, which a check on
  2026-11-18 held for him. By this ruling's reasoning it belongs with the line already on those
  pages, but it was not put to him.
- **The rest of open question 7**: who checks the work, how sessions may change records, the
  part other developers' models play, and whether AI models can put entries forward.

Nothing is published or deployed.
